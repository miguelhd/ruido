# Ruido — Architecture & Product Decision Log (ADRs)

This file tracks foundational engineering and UX decisions for **Ruido**. Append new records chronologically when making significant changes; mark superseded decisions rather than deleting history.

## ADR-001: Target Hardware & Cross-Platform Simulator-First Architecture
- **Date:** 2026-10-29 · **Status:** Accepted
- **Context:** Target hardware is the **M-VAVE FM-1** (`JieLi AC791N / pi32v2`, ~578 KB SRAM, ~600 KB XIP Flash app slot), which requires a Linux-distributed custom Clang (`pi32v2`) toolchain, while primary development happens on macOS.
- **Decision:** Isolate 100% of DSP, control logic, and `240x240` RGB565 scanline rendering inside platform-agnostic C++17 (`src/`), wrapped by two thin targets: `platforms/sim/` (native macOS / WebAssembly simulator with real-time audio & faithful front-panel UI) and `platforms/fm1/` (Dockerized `pi32v2` firmware build).
- **Consequences:** Sub-second iteration for tuning sound quality and UX on macOS using external pedals/Eurorack before flashing `.fwsc` images, with zero divergence between simulator and hardware code.

## ADR-002: Unified `4x` Oversampled (`176.47 kHz`) End-to-End Voice & Filter Path
- **Date:** 2026-10-29 · **Status:** Accepted
- **Context:** Naive digital square waves and 44.1 kHz nonlinear filters generate non-harmonic aliasing that external fuzz, distortion, wavefolder, and resonant Eurorack modules severely amplify. Because Ruido runs only 4 continuous drone voices (no polyphonic voice-stealing or sample playback), Core 1 has ~5,440-7,250 FPU cycles per stereo frame available.
- **Decision:** Run the entire synthesis and nonlinear filtering chain—oscillators, shared-rail coupling, per-voice ZDF filters, and master bus saturation—at **`4x` oversampling (`176,472 Hz`)** in single-precision `float`, decimating once at the final stereo output via cascaded polyphase half-band filters.
- **Consequences:** Uses ~35-47% of Core 1 while eliminating audible harmonic foldback and intermodulation aliasing into external drive pedals.

## ADR-003: CMOS Schmitt-Trigger Circuit Modeling (`CD40106` Reference)
- **Date:** 2026-10-29 · **Status:** Accepted
- **Context:** Standard digital square oscillators sound sterile and phase-cancel mechanically compared to the creator's reference DIY 3-oscillator analog Schmitt-trigger synth.
- **Decision:** Model three physical CMOS traits atop PolyBLEP square generation: (1) subtle RC capacitor roof tilt, (2) independent slow `1/f` thermal pitch drift per voice, and (3) weak **shared power-rail threshold coupling** controlled hands-on by the left-panel **`ALGORITHM` encoder** (`Vdd` sag nudging neighbor hysteresis thresholds so nearly-tuned oscillators pull and lock organically).
- **Consequences:** Captures authentic DIY CMOS drone interaction with direct front-panel control over circuit coupling.

## ADR-004: Per-Oscillator MS-20 (`Korg35`) Dual `HPF -> LPF` ZDF Filters with Smart-Park Shared Resonance & Pre-Filter Drive
- **Date:** 2026-10-29 · **Status:** Accepted
- **Context:** Evaluated a single shared stereo master filter vs. 4 independent per-oscillator filters, `LP/BP/HP` mode switches vs. series `HPF -> LPF`, and `Knob 4 = Pan` vs. `Pre-Filter Drive`.
- **Decision:**
  1. Give **each of the 4 oscillators its own mono series `HPF -> LPF` Korg35 ZDF filter** prior to constant-power stereo panning (plus a nonlinear master summing bus afterward to preserve multi-voice intermodulation glue when pushed).
  2. Keep the 4-knob filter strip 100% dedicated to filter physics (`KNOB1: HP Cutoff`, `KNOB2: LP Cutoff`, `KNOB3: Shared Resonance`, `KNOB4: Pre-Filter Drive`), decoupling diode saturation grit from post-filter voice `Amplitude`, and keeping `Pan` cleanly separated as a voice-mixer property.
  3. Use **Smart-Park Taper** on Shared Resonance: resonance tapers to flat (`Q = 0.707`) when `HP` is parked at `20 Hz` or `LP` is parked at `20 kHz`, preventing unwanted sub-rumble or ultrasonic whistle while waking up twin MS-20 formant peaks when both cutoffs move inward.
- **Consequences:** Eliminates filter mode switches, keeps filter vs. mixer mental models clean, and fits comfortably under 50% of Core 1 CPU.

## ADR-005: Stateless Two-Hand Ergonomics & Silkscreen-Matched Front Panel (`Approach A`)
- **Date:** 2026-10-29 · **Status:** Accepted
- **Context:** On the physical FM-1 faceplate (`docs/assets/fm1-front-panel.png`), `KNOB1-4` and the `2x6` button matrix both sit to the **right** of the center `240x240` screen, while `MASTER`, `SELECT`, `PRESETS`, `ALGORITHM`, `OCT-`/`OCT+`, and black keys `OP1-OP6`/`PIT`/`GLO` sit to the **left / bottom-center**. Holding a right-matrix button with the left hand would cross the user's arm straight over the screen.
- **Decision:**
  1. **Approach A on `KNOB1-4`:** Idle `KNOB1-4` *always* control `Osc 1-4 Speed`.
  2. **All `KNOB1-4` Momentary Holds Live on Left/Bottom-Center Black Keys:** Hold `OP1-OP4` = Osc 1-4 Filter; Hold `GLO` (bottom key) = All 4 Filters; Hold `OP5` = Osc 1-4 Amplitude; Hold `OP6` = Osc 1-4 Pan. Hands never cross the display.
  3. **Right `2x6` Button Grid = Single-Tap Only:** Only literal single-tap labels (`PLAY/STOP`, `HOME`, `SAVE`, `LFO`) are active; unused FM/sequencer buttons stay `0%` unlit.
  4. **Left Encoders = Zero-Modifier Globals:** `ALGORITHM` = Circuit Coupling/Sag, `SELECT` = Global Stereo Spread, `PRESETS` = Continuous Snapshot Morph.
- **Consequences:** 100% unobstructed display, zero modal traps, and maximum alignment with factory silkscreen labels.

## ADR-006: Always-On Triggered Scope with `240x16` Scanline Alpha-Blended HUD
- **Date:** 2026-10-29 · **Status:** Accepted
- **Context:** Full-screen `240x240` RGB565 double-buffering would consume `112.5-225 KB` of the SoC's `578 KB` SRAM, yet switching between a scope page and parameter pages breaks visual continuity.
- **Decision:** Render the `240x240` screen continuously on Core 0 via `240x16` ping-pong scanline strips (`15.36 KB` total SRAM) over SPI DMA (~45-55 FPS). Keep the Osc-1 rising-edge-triggered stereo waveform visible 100% of the time, compositing translucent HUD overlays on touch (`~120 ms` fade-in) that dissolve back to `0%` opacity after ~1.5 s idle (`~800 ms` fade-out).
- **Consequences:** Boutique CRT aesthetic and real-time phase/interval tuning with tiny RAM footprint.

## ADR-007: Varibright Monochrome Keybed Zoning & LFO-Coupled Key Pulse
- **Date:** 2026-10-29 · **Status:** Accepted
- **Context:** Hardware verification confirmed the FM-1's 27 silicone keys and 14 buttons have individually addressable **monochrome white** LEDs with software PWM dimming (not RGB), with 4 silkscreen dots dividing the 16 white keys into four groups of 4 (`[1-4]`, `[5-8]`, `[9-12]`, `[13-16]`).
- **Decision:** Map the 4 dotted white-key groups to `Osc 1-4 Latching Mutes` (`1-4`), `Osc 1-4 Pressure Expression Pads` (`5-8`), and `8 Drone Snapshots` (`9-16`), using Monome-style varibright tiers (`0%` unused/dark, `10%` muted ember, `25%` modifier landmark, `60-100%` active) and LFO-synced pulsing below `20 Hz`.
- **Consequences:** Immediate dark-room legibility that honors the hardware's silkscreen dots and monochrome LEDs.

## ADR-008: Stereo TRS Breakout & `MONO`/`POLY` Output Routing
- **Date:** 2026-10-29 · **Status:** Accepted
- **Context:** The FM-1's 3.5mm jack is an AC-coupled stereo headphone/line output; inserting a mono TS Eurorack patch cable directly shorts the Right ring to ground, while pedal rigs alternate between mono fuzz chains and stereo delay/reverb setups.
- **Decision:** Design stereo panning (`L`/`R`) assuming a **3.5mm TRS stereo breakout** cable, and bind the silkscreened `MONO` / `POLY` black keys to instant clickless **Dual-Mono** vs. **Full Stereo** output routing.
- **Consequences:** Protects the hardware output stage and adapts cleanly to both mono pedalboards and stereo/Eurorack setups.

## ADR-009: `SARADC` Carbon-Pill Polyphonic Keybed Pressure Expression
- **Date:** 2026-10-29 · **Status:** Accepted (updated by ADR-013: pitch bend removed; strictly Filter Accent + Vactrol LPG)
- **Context:** Community hardware research demonstrated that sampling `AC791N` `SARADC` channels across the silicone key matrix rows reads the analog resistance drop of the keys' conductive carbon pills under finger force without hardware modification.
- **Decision:** Integrate continuous per-key pressure sensing on Core 0 to drive **timbral & dynamic expression without ever altering oscillator frequency**: (1) sweeping MS-20 filter formant/cutoff, resonance, and diode drive on active voices or swelling muted voices through a Buchla-style Vactrol Low-Pass Gate (`LPG`) on White Keys `5-8`, and (2) momentary **rubber-band snapshot morphing** when leaning into Snapshot White Keys `9-16`.
- **Consequences:** Turns inexpensive rubber dome keys into a deeply expressive tactile filter/LPG surface while keeping oscillator pitch 100% locked to the knobs.

## ADR-010: `uint32` Phase Accumulators, Fused Single-Division Korg35 ZDF Solver & Linear-Phase Polyphase Half-Band FIR Decimation
- **Date:** 2026-10-29 · **Status:** Accepted
- **Context:** Implementing Milestone 1 exposed three subtle real-world numerical & acoustic trade-offs at `176.472 kHz` (`4x` oversampling) on a single-precision (`float32`) embedded FPU:
  1. At `0.02 Hz`, `dt = 1.13e-7`, whereas `ULP(1.0f)` in `float32` is `5.96e-8`—causing `float32` `[0, 1)` phase accumulators to suffer massive quantization error near `1.0f`.
  2. Standard 2-step Newton-Raphson resolution of two Korg35 diode-clipped Sallen-Key stages (`HPF -> LPF`) across 4 voices at `4x` OS requires up to 256 serial `fdiv` instructions per stereo frame unless algebraically reduced.
  3. Minimum-phase / allpass IIR half-band decimators introduce phase dispersion near `20 kHz` that tilts flat square-wave roofs and inflates peak crest factor (`~1.35-1.50`), eating headroom into external pedals.
- **Decision:**
  1. Drive all 4 Schmitt oscillators with **32-bit unsigned integer (`uint32_t`) phase accumulators** (`41 uHz` resolution, immune to `float32` ULP stall) paired with **4-point Integrated Cubic B-Spline PolyBLEP** (`~-108.5 to -112.6 dBc` alias floor `< 20 kHz`).
  2. Place the Padé diode clipper `phi(y) = y*(27 + z^2)/(27 + 9*z^2)` and its exact derivative `((27 - 3*z^2)/(27 + 9*z^2))^2` over a single common denominator `D^2 - alpha*k*Nd^2`, collapsing each Korg35 ZDF Newton-Raphson iteration into **one `fdiv`** (with Van der Pol period elongation compensation above `k = 2.0`).
  3. Use a **two-stage cascaded Linear-Phase Symmetric Polyphase Half-Band FIR Decimator** (`P=8` 31-tap Stage 1 + `P=32` 127-tap Stage 2, Kaiser-windowed) with double-mirrored unit-stride odd rings and 4-way unrolled accumulators—preserving `0°` phase dispersion (`1.159` isolated square crest factor) and `0.0005 dB` passband flatness.
- **Consequences:** Exceeds the `-70 dB` alias gate by `~38 dB` (`-108.5 dBc` worst-case), tracks self-oscillation within `< 0.6%` up to `8 kHz`, keeps total `DroneEngine` SRAM at `27.36 KB` (including the 24 KB scope buffer), and cuts filter division pressure by `4x-8x` on `pi32v2`.

## ADR-011: Freestanding Zero-`libm` WebAssembly Dual-Instance Simulator & Native `CoreAudio` Host
- **Date:** 2026-10-29 · **Status:** Accepted
- **Context:** Running the exact C++17 firmware engine in the browser without installing heavy external SDKs (`emsdk`) or requiring `SharedArrayBuffer` HTTP isolation headers (`COOP`/`COEP`) requires avoiding host `libc`/`libm` imports and decoupling hard real-time DSP from main-thread DOM/Canvas rendering.
- **Decision:**
  1. Replace remaining `<cmath>` calls across `src/` with deterministic IEEE-754 polynomial helpers (`fastExp`, `fastLog2`, `__builtin_fabsf`) compiled under `-ffp-contract=off` and tiny freestanding standard C++17 headers (`platforms/sim/wasm_libc/`), allowing stock Clang `--target=wasm32 -nostdinc -nostdinc++ -nostdlib` to emit a self-contained `~97 KB` `ruido.wasm` binary in `< 1 s`.
  2. Mirror the `AC791N` dual-core split in the browser by instantiating `ruido.wasm` inside an `AudioWorkletProcessor` (`Core 1` `DroneEngine`) and on the UI thread (`Core 0` `RuidoController` + `ScopeHudRenderer` rendering `15 x [240x16]` RGB565 scanline strips), exchanging `160`-byte `EngineControlState` packets and `ScopeFrame` batches via `MessagePort` transferables, alongside a native macOS `AudioToolbox` (`AudioQueue`) `44,118 Hz` live runner (`platforms/sim/macos_sim.cpp`).
- **Consequences:** Zero external SDK dependencies for Milestone 2, zero main-thread audio dropouts, `1:1` USB-MIDI `F3-G5` (`53-79`) + Aftertouch hardware controller mapping, and `43.6 KB` total combined Core 0 + Core 1 portable engine SRAM (`28,416 B` DSP + `14,808 B` UI + `448 B` Controller).

## ADR-012: Pure Monochrome Analog Tektronix CRT Scope, 4-Bit Sans-Serif ROM Typography & Unified Divider-Free HUD
- **Date:** 2026-10-29 · **Status:** Accepted (refines visual design of ADR-006)
- **Context:** Auditioning the initial Milestone 2 simulator revealed four UX shortcomings: (1) colored cyan/amber chrome and 1-bit `5x7` bitmap pixel fonts looked rough and toy-like compared to boutique hardware; (2) integer-sample vertical min/max column spans produced jagged stair-stepped waveforms; (3) boxed cards, divider lines, and colloquial labels (`"SPEED"`, `"DRONE"`) cluttered the `240x240` screen; and (4) unassigned keys gave zero visual confirmation when pressed. Since `Core 0` is 100% dedicated to UI/scanning on the dual-core `AC791N` (`~960` cycles/pixel available at 50 FPS), ample CPU headroom exists on `Core 0` without touching `Core 1` DSP.
- **Decision:**
  1. **100% Monochromatic B&W Aesthetic:** Pitch-black (`#000000`) background and strictly neutral grayscale across both the `240x240` TFT (`R5 == B5`, `G6 == (R5 << 1) | (R5 >> 4)` with `2x2` ordered Bayer dithering) and the web simulator chassis.
  2. **Analog Tektronix Dual-Beam CRT (`Option A`):** Sub-sample zero-crossing trigger lock + 4-point Catmull-Rom interpolation, 2D capsule distance Gaussian beam + `1 / sqrt(1 + slope^2)` dwell-time velocity shading + optical phosphor halo, and 6-frame `Q8.6` decaying phosphor persistence trails (`22,560 B` total `ScopeHudRenderer` SRAM, zero framebuffer).
  3. **4-Bit Anti-Aliased Sans-Serif ROM Font (`src/ui/FontSansAA.h`):** Offline-baked `HelveticaNeue-Medium` (`10pt` & `13pt` with tabular numerals `'0'..'9'`, `4.75 KB` `constexpr` XIP Flash, `0 B` SRAM).
  4. **Unified Divider-Free HUD & Full-Surface Technical Feedback:** Constant `y = 120` wave scale (zero vertical wave compression when HUD opens), soft feathered vertical vignettes with zero divider lines, `200 ms` `smoothstep01` S-curve fade-in, technical terminology throughout (`FREQUENCY`, `AUDIO RANGE` / `SUB-AUDIO LFO`, `RAIL COUPLING`, `STEREO WIDTH`), 11th black key bound to momentary **Scope Freeze**, and HUD feedback on every key, switch, encoder, and pot.
- **Consequences:** Crisp boutique monochrome instrument aesthetic, rock-solid sub-pixel analog CRT trace, `51.4 KB` total combined Core 0 + Core 1 engine SRAM (`28,416 B` DSP + `22,560 B` UI + `456 B` Controller), and immediate visual confirmation for all 27 keys and 14 buttons.

## ADR-013: `SEL` Brightness-Coupled Pressure Routing & Dual-State MS-20 Filter Accent / Vactrol LPG (`Keys 5–8`)
- **Date:** 2026-10-29 · **Status:** Accepted (supersedes `PIT` pitch-pressure toggle in ADR-009)
- **Context:** Auditioning `White Keys 5–8` exposed three UX and DSP issues with the original `PIT` black-key toggle: (1) placing a latching mode toggle on `PIT` violated the bottom black-key row's universal momentary-hold grammar (`OP1–OP6`, `GLO`, `Scope Freeze`); (2) having `PIT` dark (`0%`) in Filter mode made a live control look dead/unassigned and failed to communicate any link to `Keys 5–8`; and (3) in Filter mode, default `LPF = 20 kHz` clamped upward cutoff multiplication so pressure was barely audible on boot.
- **Decision:**
  1. Move `Keys 5–8` mode selection off the momentary black-key row (`PIT` becomes `0%` dark / `UNASSIGNED`) onto **`SEL`** in the right-hand `2x6` single-tap utility grid (`PRESSURE TARGET`: `MS-20 FILTER` <-> `PITCH BEND`), and **visually couple `SEL` to `Keys 5–8` via identical non-zero PWM brightness** (`24%` shared rest glow in Filter/LPG mode, `72%` shared rest glow in Pitch Bend mode, stepping in unison on tap, plus `SEL` flaring to `100%` alongside any pressed pad).
  2. Drive `White Keys 5–8` through an asymmetric `VactrolSmoother` (`4.5 ms` attack / `26 ms` photoconductive release): in default Filter/LPG mode (`0.000 Hz` pitch shift), active voices open `LPF` (`+4 oct`) when darkened or sweep a resonant `HPF -> LPF` Korg35 formant peak + diode drive when `LPF` is parked wide open, while muted voices swell via a Buchla-style Vactrol Low-Pass Gate (`95 Hz` sub murmur -> full harmonics); in Pitch Bend mode (`SEL` at `72%`), pressure bends pitch upward up to `+6` semitones with live `Hz` HUD tracking.
- **Consequences:** Every row on the faceplate obeys one consistent interaction rule (black-key modifiers strictly momentary, right grid strictly single-tap), monochrome white LEDs create an unmistakable Gestalt link between `SEL` and `Keys 5–8` without ever looking "off", and default Filter/LPG mode guarantees `0.000 Hz` pitch shift with dramatic out-of-the-box MS-20 expression.

## ADR-014: Contiguous `OP1–OP5` Filter Modifier Group, `GLO` Amplitude Swap & Strictly Momentary Hold Release
- **Date:** 2026-10-29 · **Status:** Accepted (updates modifier assignments in ADR-006)
- **Context:** Auditioning the left-hand black modifier keys revealed two ergonomic friction points: (1) `Global Filter` was separated onto `GLO` across `[OP6][PIT]` instead of sitting directly beside `OP1–OP4`; and (2) `OP1–OP4` felt like latching toggles because `syncModifierHud()` left `HudMode::VoiceFilter` frozen at `100%` opacity for `1.5 s` after finger release (and the web simulator included accidental `Shift+Click`, right-click `contextmenu`, and keyboard `click` latching hooks).
- **Decision:**
  1. Group **all 5 filter holds** contiguously on **`OP1–OP5`** (`OP1–OP4` = `Osc 1–4 Filter`, `OP5` = `Global Filter`, all sharing a `15%` rest LED glow) and move **`Osc 1–4 Amplitude`** to **`GLO`** (paired with `OP6` = `Panning` at `25%` landmark LED glow).
  2. Make all modifier black keys (`OP1–OP6`, `GLO`, `Scope Freeze`) strictly momentary: on release, `RuidoController::syncModifierHud` immediately exits the modifier HUD mode and begins smooth exponential dissolve (`holdRemainingSec_ = 0.0f` rather than a `1.5 s` freeze), and all simulator latching hooks are removed in favor of clean `pointerup` / `pointercancel` / `lostpointercapture` / `blur` release handling.
- **Consequences:** Cohesive 5-key filter cluster (`1–5`) + 2-key mix cluster (`6`, `G`), matched LED brightness tiers per functional group, and crisp, unmistakable momentary-hold release feedback.

## ADR-015: Extreme Self-Oscillating `Korg35` Taper & Multi-Stage Buchla/Serge `C^1` Wavefolder `DRIVE` Stage
- **Date:** 2026-10-29 · **Status:** Accepted (upgrades filter saturation & resonance staging in ADR-002 & ADR-010)
- **Context:** Auditioning `OP1–OP5` filter controls revealed two sonic limitations in the initial `Ms20DualFilter` staging: (1) `DRIVE` (`KNOB4`) sounded nearly inaudible because applying a symmetric pre-clipper (`padeClip`) to an already flat-topped `±1.0` square wave merely rescaled a constant `±1.0` into `±const` and then attenuated `postDriveScale` down to `0.953` *before* the filter—starving the `Korg35` feedback diodes; and (2) the filter felt tame because wide Smart-Park deadbands (`20–36 Hz` and `16.5–20 kHz`), a late self-oscillation threshold (`58%`, `kMax = 3.20`), unity passband gain drowning out `±1` diode limit cycles, and slow `0.010 oct/tick` cutoff stepping muted resonant drama.
- **Decision:** Push both `RES` and `DRIVE` all the way into extreme MS-20 self-oscillation and West-Coast wavefolding territory within the `176.472 kHz` `4x` oversampled loop:
  1. **Aggressive `Korg35` Self-Oscillation & Tight Smart-Park:** Raise `kMax` to `3.72` (`alpha * k <= 0.93 < 1.0`) with a concave taper (`self-oscillation` by `~35%`, screaming howl above `65%`), tighten Smart-Park to `20..24 Hz` (`HPF`) and `18.8..20 kHz` (`LPF`), add physical `Korg35` input/output resonance gain staging (`resInScale` / `resOutScale`) so resonant peaks tower above the dry square wave, recalibrate `compensateSelfOscPitch` (`0.0%` error at `200 Hz` & `1 kHz`, `< 0.5%` at `8 kHz`), and double cutoff encoder speed (`0.022 oct/tick`).
  2. **4-Stage `DRIVE` Overdrive + Division-Free `C^1` Sine Wavefolder:** When `DRIVE > 0`, (a) shape flat square roofs through a two-stage RC sharkfin contour (`95 Hz` roof integrator -> `2.4 kHz..1.1 kHz` edge slew) so every half-cycle sweeps smoothly across folds even when `HP = 20 Hz, LP = 20 kHz` are wide open while bandlimiting step edges before folding; (b) slam `1.0x -> 6.0x` unattenuated gain into `Korg35 HPF -> ±10V asymmetric buffer -> Korg35 LPF`; (c) crossfade into a division-free `C^1` polynomial Buchla/Serge multi-stage wavefolder (`wavefoldBuchlaC1`, folding across `±1, ±3, ±5...` with bounded even-harmonic bias); and (d) block asymmetric DC drift via a `9.5 Hz` output coupling capacitor (`+16 B` per voice, `Ms20DualFilter = 40 B`).
- **Consequences:** Immediate `6x` West-Coast wavefolding on `DRIVE` even with parked filters (`22 -> 132` zero-crossings on a `110 Hz` square at `0.717` RMS), ferocious pitch-accurate `Korg35` self-oscillation and chaotic diode ducking on `RES`, `< -108 dBc` clean alias floor preserved at `DRIVE = 0`, `< 0.0001` steady-state DC offset, and `18.8x` real-time single-core throughput (`1,207 ns/frame`).

## ADR-016: Mute Amplitude Memory + `GLO` Mixer `0%` Display & Clickless Fade-In Unmute
- **Date:** 2026-10-29 · **Status:** Accepted
- **Context:** Toggling `White Keys 1–4` remembers each oscillator's pre-mute `amplitude` (which also sets the ceiling for `White Keys 5–8` Vactrol LPG swells while muted), but previously holding `GLO` (`Amplitude` mixer) still showed that stored percentage (e.g. `65%`) on muted silent channels and kept them muted if turned.
- **Decision:**
  1. Keep `VoiceParams::amplitude` intact when muting/unmuting via `White Keys 1–4`, while `ScopeHudRenderer` (`KnobBankMode::Amplitude`) displays **`0%`** (`norm = 0.0f`) for any muted oscillator when viewing the `GLO` mixer.
  2. In `RuidoController::onEncoderDelta` (`HeldModifier::Amplitude`), turning a muted oscillator's knob clockwise (`ticks > 0`) clears `muted = false` and fades `amplitude` up cleanly from `0.0f` (while accidental counter-clockwise ticks at `0%` preserve both mute and remembered amplitude).
  3. In `DroneEngine::updateTargetsFromState`, when a silent closed-gate voice (`vactrolGate <= 1e-4` and `pressure <= 1e-4`) unmutes, snap `amplitude` to its new target at zero gain before opening `vactrolGate` so fading in from `0%` never produces a transient volume hump.
- **Consequences:** Gate toggles preserve exact mix levels, the `GLO` mixer truthfully reflects `0%` silence on muted channels, and turning any muted channel's knob upward smoothly fades it into the drone from `0%` and lights its gate key active.

## ADR-017: Momentary Linear 3-Speed X4 Quadrature Encoders & `C^1` Log-Octave Potentiometer Smoothing
- **Date:** 2026-10-29 · **Status:** Accepted (replaces octave-shift & velocity-ballistics encoder model from ADR-006)
- **Context:** Auditioning rotary sweeps exposed three issues: (1) discrete `24-detent` jumps smoothed with a `5.0 ms` linear-`Hz` one-pole filter settled within `15 ms` and sat on flat chromatic plateaus between detents (while rushing bass octaves `20x` faster than treble), producing audible stair-stepping; (2) velocity-based acceleration made `STANDARD` speed feel rubbery and inconsistent; and (3) `OCT-` / `OCT+` needed to follow Ruido's strictly momentary modifier rule.
- **Decision:**
  1. **Strictly Momentary `OCT-` / `OCT+` with Zero Velocity Acceleration (`96` X4 quadrature transitions = `360°`):** Remove velocity acceleration completely so angle-to-parameter response is `100%` linear and deterministic in every gear (`Released = Standard`: `~2.5–2.8` turns full sweep; `Hold OCT- = Fine`: `0.66 cents/step` audio freq, `0.09%/step` linear; `Hold OCT+ = Fast`: **`~1.30–1.33` turns** full end-to-end sweep across every parameter, chordable with `OP1–OP6` / `GLO`).
  2. **2-Pole Critically-Damped (`C^1`) Log-Octave & Linear Potentiometer Smoothers (`ParameterSmoother.h`):** Smooth `freqHz`, `hpCutoffHz`, and `lpCutoffHz` in `log2(Hz)` octave space (`LogFreqSmoother`) and continuous linear controls via `CriticallyDampedSmoother` (`14 ms` per pole, `~28 ms` mean S-curve glide, `0%` overshoot, exact settled `targetHz` caching at `0` stationary FLOPs), eliminating `t = 0` slope kinks, bass-octave rushing, and inter-step plateaus.
- **Consequences:** Liquid, stepless analog-potentiometer sweeps on every knob, predictable 1:1 linear rotation speed in `Standard`, 1.3-turn full-range throws while holding `OCT+`, surgical sub-Hz beating while holding `OCT-`, and zero latched state.

## ADR-018: Open Edge-to-Edge Coupled 4-Voice Caustic Ribbon Scope & Quarter-Res Separable Bloom
- **Date:** 2026-10-29 · **Status:** Accepted (upgrades waveform renderer in ADR-012)
- **Context:** While the 2-beam Tektronix CRT trace in `ADR-012` rendered clean stereo contours, Ruido's sonic heart is `4` coupled Schmitt oscillators driving `4` independent MS-20 (`Korg35`) filters + wavefolders. We wanted a silky, multi-strand caustic ribbon waveform where overlapping circuit trajectories flare organically into incandescent white—while staying strictly faithful to real acoustic/circuit state (`AGENTS.md` Priority #3), keeping `Core 1` DSP untouched, avoiding any `115.2 KB` `240x240` framebuffer, and fitting well within `Core 0`'s actual `~83–111` cycles/pixel budget at `50 FPS` (`240–320 MHz / 50 / 57,600`; correcting the `960` cycles/pixel typo in `ADR-012`). Interactive visual prototyping (`waveform_comparison_lab.html`) also revealed two critical UX insights: (1) plotting `Osc 1` in 100% isolation while triggering on `Osc 1` caused `Osc 1`'s strands to freeze like a static stencil; and (2) pinching wave endpoints to `y = 120` compressed outer gain-staging readability compared to Ruido's continuous open edge-to-edge drone grammar.
- **Decision:**
  1. **Sample-Aligned 16-Bit 4-Voice `ScopeFrame` Taps & Rising-Edge Mask (`20 B`):** Extend `ScopeFrame` with `int16_t voiceQ13[4]` (`[-3.5, +3.5]` @ `1/8192` = `0.0095 px` sub-pixel precision, eliminating `0.93 px` `int8` roof chatter) and `uint8_t voiceRisingMask` delayed by `36` base frames inside `DroneEngine` so all 4 voice taps and rising edges align sample-accurately with the linear-phase FIR decimated stereo output.
  2. **4096-Sample Multi-Cycle Super-Period Trigger & Continuous Phosphor Persistence (`Option A`):** Store `4096` compact `12 B` `Q3.12` scope samples (`~93 ms` @ `44.1 kHz`, guaranteeing `100%` trigger lock down to `20 Hz` and `4+` full cycles at `55 Hz`), refine each oscillator period marker to its exact continuous post-MS-20 upward zero-crossing (compensating filter group delay), match up to `8` candidate rising edges against a `48`-tap per-voice reference signature so `3:2` fifths (`55 + 82.4 Hz`) and `4:3` fourths lock cleanly onto their multi-cycle super-period with zero `30 Hz` alternating-cycle flip (`0.107 / 255` consecutive-frame delta), and apply continuous `38–135 ms` exponential phosphor slewing across `14` open edge-to-edge (`W(x) = 1.0`) strands (`3` per active voice + `2` stereo ribs).
  3. **Tile-Culled Linear Photon Accumulator, `25%` `60x60` Separable Bloom & `4x4` Bayer RGB565:** Cull empty column regions in `2` integer comparisons per `8`-px horizontal tile, accumulate `64`-entry LUT Gaussian capsules in linear photon space (`<= 8`-row sub-strips = `5.76 KB` stack), splat analytical photon energy into a `60x60` (`7.2 KB`) quarter-res grid blurred in-place via two separable `[1, 3, 4, 3, 1] / 12` passes (`25%` bloom calibration) and exact eighth-step integer bilinear upsampling, tonemap via a `1024`-entry `constexpr` soft-knee Reinhard + `0.82` gamma LUT (`1.12 KB` XIP Flash), and dither to neutral `RGB565` via a `4x4` Bayer matrix.
- **Consequences:** Calm, silky 3D caustic ribbon acoustics reflecting every individual voice mute, filter sweep, resonance peak, wavefold, natural beating, and LPG swell without scope-trigger jitter; zero `240x240` framebuffer (`ScopeHudRenderer = 79.28 KB`, `DroneEngine = 44.75 KB`, combined `< 125 KB` of `578 KB` SRAM); `100%` bit-identical `8`-, `16`-, and `24`-row strip equivalence; and `< 21%` estimated `Core 0` load on `AC791N`.

## ADR-019: Independent MS-20 High-Pass & Low-Pass Resonance (`HPF | HP RES | LPF | LP RES`) & Unassigned `LFO` Key Reservation

- **Consequences:** Authentic MS-20 sub-bass peak boosting, asymmetric vowel formants, and dual-sine self-oscillation per voice; lower Core 1 filter CPU & state footprint; cleaner HUD curve correspondence; and a clean slate on the `LFO` button for a dedicated modulation LFO later.

## ADR-022: Dedicated Per-Voice LFO Specification (`SELECT` = Voice, `TARGET | SHAPE | RATE | DEPTH`)
- **Date:** 2026-10-30 · **Status:** Implemented (supersedes unassigned `LFO` placeholder in ADR-019)
- **Context:** Ruido consists of 4 independent mono synth voices (`4` oscillators, `4` MS-20 dual filters, `4` VCAs, `4` panners). A single shared global LFO violates that 4-voice independence, while multi-slot mod matrices with cross-mod menus violate Ruido's immediate analog hardware spirit. With `ButtonId::Lfo` operating as a latching page switch and `EncoderId::Select` freed (`ADR-021`), each voice can own a dedicated, hands-on LFO with zero sub-menus.
- **Decision (Locked Specification):**
  1. **1 Dedicated LFO Per Voice (`4` Independent Voice LFOs):** Each voice (`Osc 1..4`) owns one dedicated LFO (`uint32_t` phase accumulator + smooth drift generator running at audio/frame rate on Core 1).
  2. **Page Entry, Exit & Voice Selection:** Tapping **`LFO`** (`ButtonId::Lfo`) enters the **LFO Page**; tapping `LFO` again or pressing **`HOME`** returns to the pure scope while active LFOs continue running. While on the LFO Page, turning **`SELECT`** (`EncoderId::Select`) steps across **`OSC 1` · `OSC 2` · `OSC 3` · `OSC 4` · `ALL 4`**.
  3. **Ergonomic Knob Order (`TARGET | SHAPE | RATE | DEPTH` — Setup Left, Perform Right):**
     - **`KNOB 1` — `TARGET` (7 Stepped Per-Voice Circuit Parameters):** **`LPF`** *(default)* · **`LP RES`** · **`HPF`** · **`HP RES`** · **`AMP`** · **`PITCH`** · **`PAN`** (strictly 1-to-1 with real `VoiceParams` circuit parameters).
     - **`KNOB 2` — `SHAPE` (`0–100%` Continuous Seamless Morph):** Stepless continuous morph across **`Sine` (`0%`) -> `Triangle` (`25%`) -> `Sawtooth` (`50%`) -> `Slewed Square` (`75%`, vactrol-rounded corners) -> `Smooth Drift` (`100%`, non-repeating organic wander)**.
     - **`KNOB 3` — `RATE` (`0.02 Hz – 30 Hz` Continuous Log-Octave Sweep):** `50 s` glacial drift up to `30 Hz` flutter/FM (`LogFreqSmoother`).
     - **`KNOB 4` — `DEPTH` (`0–100%` Unipolar Continuous Amount):** Hard stop at `0%` CCW so any LFO can be zeroed blindly by feel without overshooting into inverted modulation (`CriticallyDampedSmoother`).
  4. **Acoustic Visuals (`ScopeHudRenderer`):** On the LFO Page, a dedicated full-screen LFO visualization (`x = 0..239` edge-to-edge, `y = 26..188`, `±75 px` / `150 px` peak-to-peak centered on `y = 107`) renders the focused voice's live morphed LFO wave, clean gradient shading (zero decorative inner contour lines), and a crisp optical phase bead proportioned to match the MS-20 filter cutoff beads over the `72%`-dimmed background waveform (or overlays all 4 voices' LFO curves & beads when `SELECT = ALL 4`, crossfading independently with the `1/3`-screen `OP1–OP5` MS-20 filter Bode plot), while active filter LFO modulation animates the MS-20 filter curves/beads in real time whenever `OP1–OP5` is held.
- **Consequences:** Preserves 100% per-voice musical independence (e.g., rock-steady sub bass on `Osc 1` while `Osc 2–4` run independent filter swells, resonance self-oscillation tides, tremolo, or pitch/pan drift), keeps discrete choices on the left (`SELECT`, `KNOB 1`) and continuous performance sweeps (`SHAPE`, `RATE`, `DEPTH`) on the right, and requires zero nested menus.

## ADR-024: MS-20 `Rev 2` (`KLM-307` `LM13600` OTA Sallen-Key) Nodal ZDF Filter & 4-Voice Lockstep Solver
- **Date:** 2026-10-30 · **Status:** Accepted (supersedes simplified linear-pole Padé `Korg35` solver in ADR-010 and synthetic `dcMean_` tracker in ADR-019)
- **Context:** Auditing `Ms20DualFilter` against real Korg MS-20 hardware revealed three fidelity gaps in our original linear-pole Sallen-Key model: (1) both `HPF` and `LPF` integrator poles were strictly linear (`RES = 0%` behaved like a sterile textbook digital Butterworth filter with zero saturation or square-edge rounding); (2) the `[27 + z^2]/[27 + 9z^2]` Padé soft-clipper lacked the flat small-signal deadband + sharp `~0.62 V` PN-junction knee of anti-parallel `1S1588` silicon diodes; and (3) summing input $x$ linearly with diode feedback required an artificial 1-pole `dcMean_` tracker on `LPF` to hide rectified DC drift. Between MS-20 `Rev 1` (`Korg35` BJT) and **`Rev 2` (`KLM-307` `LM13600` OTA Sallen-Key)**, we selected **`Rev 2`** (`Flavor B`) for its creamy, liquid OTA saturation across drones, massive bass weight, and organic dual-OTA pole compression.
- **Decision:**
  1. **True Capacitive Sallen-Key Nodal Topology (Organic Zero DC & Zero Bass Quenching):**
     - **`HPF` (`IC5`):** Inject input $x$ capacitively into series `C1` ($v_{C1} = y_1 - x$) and buffered `C2` ($v_{C2} = y_2 - y_1$), with `OTA 1` discharging $y_1$ toward $\phi_{\text{diode}}(k_{\text{hp}} y_2)$ and `OTA 2` discharging $y_2$ toward ground—guaranteeing exact `0 Hz` capacitive DC rejection (`-156 dB` measured leak under `+0.85 V` DC bias) and eliminating `dcMean_`.
     - **`LPF` (`IC6`):** Sense `Stage 2` capacitor differential $w = y_1 - y_2 = \tilde{s} y_2$ into the `1S1588` feedback limiter $\phi_{\text{diode}}(k_{\text{lp}} w)$, yielding the exact Sallen-Key denominator $\tilde{s}^2 + (2 - k)\tilde{s} + 1$ while placing sub-cutoff bass signals (`65 Hz` squares or `220 Hz` `HPF` self-oscillation) inside $w$'s band-pass null so simultaneous `HPF + LPF` self-oscillation never suffers Van der Pol asynchronous bass quenching.
  2. **Pure-FPU $C^2$ `LM13600` OTA Transconductors on All 4 Poles & $C^4$-Deadband `1S1588` Silicon Limiters:**
     - Model all 4 poles per voice (`8` OTAs/voice, `32` OTAs total @ `176.472 kHz`) with odd quintic $C^2$-continuous `LM13600` diode-linearized transconductors $\sigma_{\text{ota}}(v) = v_c(1 - \frac{2}{3}u^2 + \frac{1}{5}u^4)$ (`\sigma' = (1 - u^2)^2 \in [0, 1]`, `~1.08` nominal saturation knee, monolithic `1.5%–2.6%` Wilson current-mirror tolerances balanced across nodes so steady-state limit-cycle DC stays `< 0.5 mV`).
     - Model both anti-parallel `1S1588` silicon feedback limiters with $C^4$-deadband / $C^2$-rail polynomials $Q(u) = u(1 - \frac{3}{5}u^4 + \frac{2}{7}u^6)$ (`Q' = (1 - u^2)^2(1 + 2u^2)`, zero $u^3$ term for liquid small-signal Q, plus `0.024f` bulk ohmic slope and `kMax = 3.68`), avoiding GPR<->FPU `memcpy` bit-hack stalls under `-ffp-contract=off`.
  3. **Exact 1D Reduced Analytical Newton-Raphson Solver & 4-Voice Lockstep SIMD (`processQuadBank4x`):**
     - Algebraically eliminate internal node states so all 3 nonlinearities per stage (`OTA 1`, `OTA 2`, `1S1588` diode) reduce to a 1D scalar root with proven global monotonicity (`df > 0.12` across $\mathbb{R}^2$), warm-started by continuous 2-point capacitor-state predictors (`q = y2 - x`, `w = y1 - y2`), calibrated with Groszkowski reactive-power + dual-OTA compression pitch curves, and evaluated across all 4 voices in lockstep SIMD lanes via `Ms20DualFilter::processQuadBank4x` (transparent linear Smart-Park bypass at `20 Hz` / `20 kHz`).
- **Consequences:** Authentic MS-20 `Rev 2` (`LM13600` + `1S1588`) creamy harmonic warmth even at `RES = 0%` (`H3 = -45.82 dBc`, `H2 = -61.16 dBc`, `< -108.6 dBc` alias floor), `< 0.5%` self-oscillation pitch tracking (`200 Hz` & `1 kHz`), quench-free dual `HPF(220) + LPF(880)` self-oscillation (`1.073` RMS), `-0.47 mV` steady-state DC (`1.53e-8` input DC leak) with zero post-filter DC blockers, `sizeof(Ms20DualFilter) = 32 B` (`DroneEngine = 45.50 KB`), bit-exact `0.0f` parity between `processQuadBank4x` and `processPrepared`, and **`29.0x` real-time** single-core throughput (`782.1 ns/frame`).

  3. Holding `OCT-` / `OCT+` showed ambiguous `"FINE RATE"` / `"FAST RATE"` text that immediately got overwritten as soon as any knob turned, and scaled discrete notched detents (`SELECT`, `LFO TARGET`) unevenly across gears.
  4. Multi-voice `"ALL"` controls were scattered opaquely between `OP5` (`FILTER GLOBAL`) and a 5th `SELECT` slot inside `LFO`, leaving no unified Elektron `CTRL-ALL`-style multi-voice performance surface.
  5. Right-grid `GLO` needed a scalable vertical configuration menu architecture rather than a 4-knob fixed bank.
- **Decision:**

  2. **Centralized `150 ms` S-Curve HUD Envelope (`HudEnvelope.h`):** Unify **all** HUD fade-ins and fade-outs (`kHudFadeSec = 0.150f`, `kHudToastHoldSec = 1.500f`, smoothstep `u^2(3 - 2u)`) across controller opacity, `HOME` LED, all 5 center graph envelopes, and the `72%` waveform backdrop dim.
  3. **Option A `FINE` / `COARSE` Split Top-Bar Override & Gear-Invariant Detents:** Rename `KnobRate::Fast` -> `KnobRate::Coarse`; lock the top-left HUD header to **`FINE`** (`OCT-`) or **`COARSE`** (`OCT+`) for the entire physical button hold while updating the top-right slot live with high-precision value readouts (`OSC 1  110.04 Hz`, `LPF  1.42 kHz`, `MASTER  85%`); and keep discrete notched selectors (`SELECT`, `LFO TARGET`, `GLOBAL CONFIG`) strictly `1` item per physical detent (`4` X4 ticks) across all 3 gears.
  4. **`OP5 = Amplitude` & 5-Mode Keybed `GLOBAL` (`GLO + SELECT`):** Move per-voice `Amplitude` to `OP5` (`25%` landmark rest LED alongside `OP6 = Panning`), restrict the `LFO` button page `SELECT` strictly to `OSC 1..4`, and make holding keybed **`GLO`** (`25%` rest LED) enter **`GLOBAL`**, where **`SELECT`** chooses across 5 destinations (`All` default on boot, last choice remembered), each with custom acoustic center graphs and 4-voice spread micro-beads on diverged bottom filaments:
     - **`GLOBAL FREQ` (`PITCH | SPREAD | DETUNE | TILT`):** Mutually orthogonal 4-voice log-octave basis (`mean`, symmetric `{ -1.5, -0.5, +0.5, +1.5 }` span, zero-sum `{ -1, +1, -1, +1 }` pair beat, and span-independent spectral tilt, with fallback unison voices) + `20 Hz–4 kHz` log-frequency spectral stems graph.
     - **`GLOBAL FILTER` (`HPF | HP RES | LPF | LP RES`):** Relative log-octave / linear deltas across all 4 MS-20 dual filters + 4-voice overlaid MS-20 passband curves.
     - **`GLOBAL LFO` (`TARGET | SHAPE | RATE | DEPTH`):** Multi-voice LFO macro (`"MIXED"` readout when shapes/targets diverge) + full-screen 4-voice morphed LFO waves & 4 phase beacons.
     - **`GLOBAL AMP` (`LEVEL | TILT | WIDTH | BALANCE`):** Relative VCA level, low-to-high voice balance tilt, stereo width expansion/collapse around pan centroid, and stereo shift + `L..C..R` stereo soundstage horizon & 4 voice pillars.
     - **`GLOBAL ALL` (`FREQ | FILTER | LFO | AMP`):** Single-gesture 4-stage macro (`Pitch`, dual `LPF+HPF` open/close, `LFO Depth`, `Voice Level`) + unified `20 Hz–20 kHz` MS-20 passband overlaid with the 4 live oscillator pitch/level stems.
  5. **Scalable `GLOBAL CONFIG` Menu (Right-Grid `GLO`) & Bug Fixes:** Right-grid `GLO` (`15%` rest / `100%` open LED) toggles a vertical `5`-row scrolling list menu (`SELECT` moves row cursor, any `KNOB 1–4` steps highlighted value, bottom 4-knob strip hidden), shipping with **`GLO RELEASE`** (`LATCH` default vs Elektron-style `SNAP BACK` restoring `4 x VoiceParams` on `GLO` release) and **`OUTPUT ROUTING`** (`STEREO` / `DUAL MONO`). Also fixes stale `focusedKnob` column highlights (`kNoFocusedKnob`), `LFO` `15%` rest LED, `PRESETS ENCODER · UNASSIGNED` toast, `SAVE` 2nd-tap `"CANCELLED"` toast, and slow-sweep `MASTER` pot wake hysteresis (`0.2%`).
- **Consequences:** Every handwritten `src/` file stays `<= 670/700` lines (`41` files, `[Hygiene OK]`), `sizeof(RuidoController) = 720 B`, `sizeof(ScopeHudRenderer) = 85.85 KB`, Wasm code+data = `243 KB` (`< 41%` of `600 KB` XIP Flash ceiling), `37.0x` real-time DSP throughput, zero UBSan/TSan issues, and 100% consistent interaction rules across hardware controls and simulator.

## ADR-028: Unclamped Circuit Extremes (`0.05 Hz–20 kHz` Osc, `2 Hz–28 kHz` `Rev 2 -> Korg35` MS-20, Starved-Rail Sync, Audio-Rate LFOs) & Latching `EDIT` Channel Level/Drive Page (`HudAmpGraph`)

- **Context:** Ruido is designed as an uncompromising analog-modeled drone instrument of sonic extremes rather than a polite studio-clamped synth. - **Consequences:** Zero artificial musical walls while maintaining `< -108.6 dBc` alias suppression, `< 2.5 mV` steady-state DC, bounded `<= 1.45` DAC output under full ultrasonic self-oscillation torture, **`30.7x` real-time** single-core DSP throughput (`737 ns/frame`, `sizeof(DroneEngine) = 45.69 KB`), `sizeof(ScopeHudRenderer) = 85.97 KB`, `44` handwritten `src/` files `<= 682/700` lines (`[Hygiene OK]`), `272.3 KB` Wasm (`< 46%` of `600 KB` XIP Flash ceiling), and 0 UBSan/TSan issues.

## ADR-029: Latching Right-Grid `FX` MS-20 Filter Page, Unassigned `OP1–OP4` & `SEL`, Smooth Diagnostic Mute/Sub-Audio Key LEDs, and Divider-Free Chrome
- **Date:** 2026-10-30 · **Status:** Implemented (supersedes `OP1–OP4` momentary filter holds in ADR-006/ADR-019, `SEL` pressure-target toggle in ADR-011/ADR-023, and static/gate-blink `Keys 1–4` LEDs in ADR-007/ADR-028)
- **Context:** After moving Channel Level & MS-20 Overdrive onto the right-grid `EDIT` page (`ADR-028`) alongside `LFO` and `GLOBAL CONFIG`, keeping Per-Voice MS-20 Dual Filters as 4 separate momentary holds on `OP1–OP4` felt ergonomically asymmetric: deep two-handed dual-resonant `HPF + LPF` formant sculpting benefits from a hands-free latching page just like `EDIT` and `LFO`, while `FX` sat unassigned right below `KNOB 1`. Simultaneously, `SEL`'s Pitch-Bend pressure toggle was unneeded (`White Keys 5–8` work best as dedicated MS-20 Filter Accent / Vactrol LPG pads); decorative lines (`FINE`/`COARSE` underline in `HudChrome`, unselected row separators and left vertical bar in `GLOBAL CONFIG`) violated Ruido's divider-free typography rule; and raw Schmitt square-gate LED blinking below `20 Hz` on `White Keys 1–4` felt harsh while a static `10%` Muted LED failed to catch the eye when diagnosing an inaudible oscillator.
- **Decision:**
  1. **Latching Right-Grid `FX` Filter Page (`ButtonId::Fx`):** Map `FX` to the **Per-Voice MS-20 Dual Filter Page** (`HudMode::VoiceFilter`, `KnobBankMode::VoiceFilter`) following the exact standardized right-grid page pattern established by `LFO` and `EDIT`:
     - Mutually exclusive with `EDIT`, `LFO`, `GLOBAL CONFIG`, and `SAVE`; second `FX` tap or `HOME` unlatches and dissolves in `150 ms` back to pure scope (`KNOB 1–4 = Frequency`), while momentary holds (`OP6` Panning, Keybed `GLO`, `ScopeFreezeKey`, `White Keys 5–16`) temporarily override and seamlessly restore `FX` on release.
     - **`SELECT`** steps `fxVoiceFocus` (`OSC 1..4`, `1` item per `4`-tick detent across all 3 gears, clamped with zero windup, remembered across page re-opens); **`KNOB 1–4`** adjust `HPF | HP RES | LPF | LP RES` for `voices[fxVoiceFocus]` alongside the full-screen (`x = 0..239` edge-to-edge, `y = 26..186`, `241` fenceposts, `0 dB` at `y = 86` with `60 px` `+18 dB` `Korg35` horn headroom and `100 px` passband skirt; supersedes the `1/3`-screen `y = 102..182` box in ADR-020) MS-20 dual-peak passband curve over a `72%`-dimmed waveform backdrop.
     - **Acoustic `FX` LED (`LedEngine.cpp`):** Glows at **`15%` rest** when all active voices' filters are parked flat (`hpCutoffHz <= 2.05 Hz`, `lpCutoffHz >= 27.9 kHz`, `0%` resonance), scales dynamically across **`15%–85%`** when any unmuted or pressure-active voice has filter cutoff/resonance engaged (computed via shared `computeLiveVoiceFilter` in `core/LfoMath.h`, including live Filter LFO and Pad Pressure sweeps), and lights to **`100%`** while the page is open.
  2. **Unassign `OP1–OP4` & `SEL` (`0%` Dark LEDs):** Free `BlackKeyId::Op1..Op4` (`OP1 KEY..OP4 KEY · UNASSIGNED`, `0%` rest LED, leaving `OP6` Panning and `GLO` 5-mode Global as the clean bottom-center momentary modifiers) and free `ButtonId::Sel` (`SEL SWITCH · UNASSIGNED`, `0%` rest LED, keeping `White Keys 5–8` dedicated to MS-20 Filter / Vactrol LPG expression at `24%` rest -> `100%` touch flare).
  3. **Smooth `C^2` Diagnostic `White Keys 1–4` LEDs (`LedEngine` + `LedSmoothState`):** Replace static `10%` Mute and harsh `< 20 Hz` Schmitt square-gate LED jumps with smooth, S-curve-transitioned analog sine indicators (`evalAnalogSine`) that clearly distinguish *why* an oscillator is inaudible without harsh edges:
     - **User-Activated Muted (`muted == true`):** Clear, attention-catching **`1.50 Hz`** `C^2` sine pulse (`4% <-> 54%`), smoothly slewed via `muteBlendSmooth` (`1 - exp(-dt * 12.5)` + `smoothstep01`).
     - **Active Below Hearing Range (`!muted`, `< 20 Hz`):** Slow, calm **`0.35 Hz`** (`~2.86 s`, `4.3x` slower) `C^2` sine breathing (`14% <-> 52% + 28% * amp`), smoothly crossfaded across `15–25 Hz` in log-octave space and temporally slewed via `subAudioBlendSmooth` (with `>= 7 Hz` fast/audio-rate LFO modulation tapered out of key LEDs so keys never strobe).
  4. **Strictly Divider-Free `FINE`/`COARSE` Badge & `GLOBAL CONFIG` List (`HudChrome.cpp`, `HudGlobalViews.cpp`):** Remove the `y = 18.8f` horizontal underline beneath `FINE`/`COARSE` and remove both the unselected row separator filaments and the leftmost vertical white slit on the selected row in `GLOBAL CONFIG`, relying purely on clean anti-aliased typography, brightness hierarchy, and subtle row-fill luminance.
- **Consequences:** All four per-voice/global right-grid pages (`FX`, `EDIT`, `LFO`, `GLO`) share one unified interaction and divider-free visual language; `White Keys 1–4` immediately and gracefully communicate Audible vs Sub-Audio (`0.35 Hz` breath) vs User-Muted (`1.50 Hz` pulse); `44` handwritten `src/` files stay `<= 666/700` lines (`[Hygiene OK]`, `core <- ui` DAG clean); `sizeof(RuidoController) = 760 B`, `sizeof(ScopeHudRenderer) = 85,968 B`, `~270 KB` Wasm (`< 45%` of `600 KB` XIP Flash ceiling), and `0` failures across DSP, UI, Wasm V8, and UBSan suites.

## ADR-030: 4-Mode `EDIT` Channel Strip (`WIDTH · DRIFT · LEVEL · PAN`), `ALGORITHM` Menu Option Scrolling, 1-to-1 Steady HUD Audibility Dimming, and `0%` Lower-Rail Auto-Mute

  1. On the latching `EDIT` page (`ADR-028`), assigning `SELECT` to a continuous 4-voice `"ALL"` gang fader broke consistency with every other page (`FX`, `LFO`, `GLOBAL`, `GLOBAL CONFIG`), where `SELECT` steps discretely between modes/targets, while per-oscillator **Pulse Width (`WIDTH`)** and **`1/f` Thermal Drift (`DRIFT`)** lacked dedicated front-panel controls alongside fused **`LEVEL` + `4.5x` MS-20 Drive** and **`PAN`**.

  3. In `HudChrome::compositeBottomKnobStrip`, `OSC 1–4` label luminance (`lblGray`) interpolated toward `255` white using raw `focusMix_[k]` without scaling by `(1 - dim)`, lighting silent/muted focused columns to `#FFFFFF` white.
  4. `computeHudGaugeTargets` only dimmed `vp.muted`, leaving `< 20 Hz` sub-audio oscillators (`0.05–20 Hz`) `100%` bright on screen while `White Keys 1–4` breathed as inaudible.
  5. Dialing `KNOB 1–4` down to the `0%` `LEVEL` lower rail left `vp.muted == false` (leaving `White Keys 1–4` steady-lit while completely silent, and leaving unmute stuck at `0%`).

  2. **`GLOBAL CONFIG` Option Scrolling on `ALGORITHM` (`RuidoController.cpp`):** When `GLOBAL CONFIG` is open (`mod == HeldModifier::None`), `SELECT` moves the row cursor (`1` row/detent) and **`ALGORITHM`** steps the highlighted row's option value (`1` option/detent via `configTickAccum_`), freeing `KNOB 1–4` so turning them adjusts `Osc 1–4 Frequency` with a `1.5 s` toast before returning to `GLOBAL CONFIG`.
  3. **Single Shared `computeVoiceAudibility` Model & 1-to-1 Steady Screen Dimming (`LfoMath.h`, `LedEngine.cpp`, `HudChrome.cpp`, `HudAmpGraph.cpp`, `HudGlobalViews.cpp`):** Centralize phase-free per-voice audibility (`subAudioBlend` across `15–25 Hz` log-octave space, `muteBlend` lifted smoothly by `Pad 5–8` LPG pressure, `inaudibleDim = max(muteBlend, subAudioBlend)`) and scale `effFoc = foc * (1.0f - dim)` across all 5 `isPerOscKnobBank` modes (`Frequency`, `PulseWidth`, `VoiceDrift`, `Amplitude`, `Panning`) so `OSC 1–4` labels, filaments, beads, and values dim steadily (`0%` screen pulsing) whenever an oscillator is muted or sub-audio.
  4. **Coherent `0%` `LEVEL` Lower-Rail Auto-Mute, `65%` Unmute Recovery & Live `PLAY/STOP` Zero-Crossing Lock (`EncoderMath.h`, `RuidoController.cpp`, `ScopeTraceEngine.cpp`):** Turning `KNOB 1–4` on `EDIT -> LEVEL` down to `<= 1.0e-4f` (without active `AMP` LFO) sets `vp.muted = true` (`amplitude = 0.0f`); turning `+1` tick CW smoothly unmutes from `0%`; tapping a pulsing `White Key 1–4` at `0%` restores `kDefaultVoiceAmp` (`65%`); and `ScopeTraceEngine` filters rising-edge candidates for positive zero-crossing slope so `PLAY/STOP` resume locks immediately onto live audio.
- **Consequences:** Every page on the machine now uses `SELECT` consistently as a discrete mode/voice/row selector while pairing `GLOBAL CONFIG` navigation + option scrolling ergonomically on `SELECT` + `ALGORITHM`; `< -109.9 dBc` narrow-pulse alias floor (`15%` duty @ `997.31 Hz`); `White Keys 1–4` and `OSC 1–4` HUD columns stay 1-to-1 synchronized with zero screen pulsing; `44` handwritten `src/` files stay `<= 680/700` lines (`[Hygiene OK]`, `core <- ui` DAG clean); `sizeof(DroneEngine) = 45.94 KB`, `sizeof(RuidoController) = 824 B`, `sizeof(ScopeHudRenderer) = 86,312 B`, `286.0 KB` Wasm (`< 48%` of `600 KB` XIP Flash ceiling, `30`-float telemetry), and `0` failures across DSP, UI, macOS headless, Wasm V8, and UBSan suites.

## ADR-031: Dual Per-Oscillator LFO Bank (`LFO A/B OSC 1..4`), `WIDTH` PWM Target, Zero-Delay `LFO B -> LFO A` Cross-Modulation & Smart `GLOBAL LFO` Scaling
- **Date:** 2026-10-30
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Context:** With `~46–59%` Core 1 CPU headroom, `~350+ KB` free SRAM, and `< 50%` XIP Flash utilization remaining on `AC791N`, giving each oscillator **two independent analog-modeled LFOs (`LFO A` + `LFO B` = `8` LFOs total)** unlocks deep organic polyrhythms, PWM timbral motion, and self-evolving modulation without compromising real-time audio margins or introducing multi-page modal traps.
- **Decision:**
  1. **8-Slot Dual Per-Oscillator LFO Bank (`VoiceLfoBank`, `Parameters.h`):** Expand `VoiceLfoBank` to `kNumVoiceLfos = 8` (`[0..3] = LFO A` for `Osc 1..4`, `[4..7] = LFO B` for `Osc 1..4`) stepped at `44,118 Hz`, stepping `[4..7]` (`LFO B`) **before** `[0..3]` (`LFO A`) so `LFO B -> LFO A` cross-modulation executes with zero sample delay.
  2. **Standardized `LFO` Page, Two-Tone `LFO A/B OSC 1..4` Header & Companion Sibling Curve (`RuidoController.cpp`, `HudChrome.cpp`, `HudGraphs.cpp`):** Keep `ButtonId::Lfo` strictly standardized **Open / Close** (`150 ms` dissolve) while `SELECT` steps sequentially (`1` slot per `4`-tick detent across all 3 gears, clamped `0..7` with zero windup) across **`LFO A OSC 1` · `LFO B OSC 1` · `LFO A OSC 2` · `LFO B OSC 2` · `LFO A OSC 3` · `LFO B OSC 3` · `LFO A OSC 4` · `LFO B OSC 4`** (rendering `"LFO A"` / `"LFO B"` as the `#FFFFFF` bold primary title and `" OSC 1"` .. `" OSC 4"` as the dimmed silver target qualifier, matching `FREQUENCY OSC 1` and `FILTER OSC 1`). When viewing any slot (`A` or `B`) on `HudGraphs`, render the focused LFO in crisp `#FFFFFF` foreground with its radiant Phase Beacon while rendering the same oscillator's active sibling (`B` or `A`, when `depth > 0.2%`) as a `~32%` silver companion trace + subtle optical phase bead, reflecting live `LFO B -> LFO A` rate/depth warp directly on `LFO A`'s curve and bottom-strip readouts.
  3. **Expanded Targets (`WIDTH` PWM + Exclusive `LFO B -> A RATE / A DEPTH`):** Add **`WIDTH`** (`LfoTarget::PulseWidth`, band-limited `5–95%` Schmitt PWM) to both `LFO A` (`kNumLfoATargets = 8`: `PITCH · WIDTH · LPF · LP RES · HPF · HP RES · AMP · PAN`) and `LFO B`, and append **`A RATE`** (`LfoTarget::LfoARate = 8`, `±6 oct` log-octave warp across `0.01 Hz–2,000 Hz`) and **`A DEPTH`** (`LfoTarget::LfoADepth = 9`, swelling/fading `LFO A` strength from stillness to crest) exclusively at the end of **`LFO B`'s** `TARGET` list (`kNumLfoBTargets = 10`), with clickless `~12.5 ms` crossfade weights across all 10 destinations and sequential/additive stacking when `A` and `B` target the same voice parameter.
  4. **Smart `GLOBAL LFO` & `GLOBAL ALL` Macro Laws (`GlobalControl.cpp`):** On `Hold GLO -> GLOBAL LFO` (`X/8 ACTIVE` / `BYPASSED`), **`RATE`** scales all `8` LFOs proportionally in log-octave space (preserving `A:B` polyrhythmic speed ratios), while **`DEPTH`** (and `GLOBAL ALL` `KNOB 3 = LFO`) scales **only currently active LFOs** (`depth > 0%`, keeping bypassed LFOs strictly silent) or wakes **`LFO A` (`OSC 1..4`)** across all 4 oscillators when turned CW from an all-`0%` state (`TARGET` steps `0..7` while preserving active `LFO B -> A RATE / A DEPTH` patches).
- **Consequences:** Doubles modulation power to `8` analog-modeled `0.01–2,000 Hz` LFOs with zero modal sub-pages; adds only `+2.37 KB` to `DroneEngine` (`47.19 KB` total, `24.8x` RT single-core throughput), `+128 B` to `RuidoController` (`952 B`), `+120 B` to `ScopeHudRenderer` (`86,432 B`), and `+13 KB` to `ruido.wasm` (`299.0 KB`, `< 50%` of `600 KB` XIP Flash ceiling, `46`-float telemetry), with all `44` handwritten `src/` files `<= 688/700` lines (`[Hygiene OK]`) and `0` failures across DSP, UI, macOS headless, Wasm V8, and UBSan suites.

## ADR-034: Composite Community Hardware Stack & Acoustic-First Bring-Up Rules (`platforms/fm1/`)
- **Date:** 2026-10-31 · **Status:** Accepted (replaces `m-vave/fm1_open` / `jlstream` assumption in ADR-001; refined by empirical toolchain & license audit in ADR-044)
- **Context:** Milestone 4 (`platforms/fm1/`) requires a Board Support Package (BSP) and hardware shell on the M-VAVE FM-1 (`JieLi AC791N` / `WL82` dual-core `pi32v2` SoC) to host Ruido's portable C++17 `src/` engine without compromising sound quality. Auditing the FM-1 open-source ecosystem and `pi32v2` hardware architecture surfaced four critical realities:
  1. **Official `m-vave/fm1_open` Links a Proprietary Groovebox Blob:** Links against closed-source `sdk/lib/libfm1_engine.a`, consuming XIP NOR Flash and internal SRAM while owning the audio pipeline.
  2. **`zvenson/dinghy` + `hugelton/felucca-bsp` (`MPL-2.0`) Provide a Clean Open Base, With Four Vanilla Gaps:** `dinghy` (`platform.c` + `hal/` derived from `felucca-bsp`) cleanly provides boot init, power-on **`Hold OCT-` USB rescue mode** (essential on single-bank `0x02000000` XIP NOR flash), non-blocking `LCD_IDLE` `SPI1` DMA strip transfers, `ALNK0` I2S + USB-C stereo audio, USB/TRS MIDI, and CRC-verified `settings_*` / `slot_*` flash storage. However, unmodified `dinghy` (a) runs audio inside a single-core `Core 0` ISR, (b) omits `-mfprev1` hardware float emission, (c) scans keys strictly as digital switches without `PA7`/`PA8` `SARADC` pressure, and (d) drives the `2 × 74HC595` LED shift registers as binary states.
  3. **`pi32v2` `float32` ISA, `__divsf3` Software Division & `-ffast-math` Hazard:** `AC791N` `r3` silicon supports single-precision FPU instructions (`fadds`, `fsubs`, `fmuls`) only when compiled with `-target pi32v2 -mcpu=r3 -mfprev1` (banning 64-bit `soft-double` and 64-bit integer division `__aeabi_ldivmod`), whereas `float / float` still lowers to software `__divsf3` (`~35–50` cycles/call, which would burn most of `Core 1` across `~108–140` divisions/frame unless reciprocals are hoisted or evaluated via 2-step Newton-Raphson). Meanwhile, enabling `-ffast-math` (`-fassociative-math -ffinite-math-only`) algebraically folds `(x + c) - c` subnormal scrubbing into a no-op and strips `NaN`/`Inf` safety guards, risking denormal CPU overruns and breaking bit-exact simulator parity.
  4. **16-Bit Fixed-Point DAC/USB Boundary, Flash XIP Contention & PCB EMI:** Unlike floating-point desktop audio, FM-1's `ALNK0` I2S DAC and USB-C UAC1 stream signed 16-bit integer PCM (`[-32768, +32767]`), USB-C audio runs on the host's SOF clock (`~±50 ppm` vs the FM-1's `24 MHz` crystal), 4 KB NOR Flash sector erases stall XIP fetches for `30–100 ms`, `SARADC` multiplexes `PA7`/`PA8` keybed rows alongside the `MASTER` pot, and `2 × 74HC595` (16 pins) time-multiplexes all 42 LEDs across scan banks (where naive 8-bit BAM requires `> 200 kHz` LSB timers, flashes on `127 <-> 128` MSB transitions, and can couple `1–4 kHz` whine into the shared analog ground plane).
- **Decision:** Build `platforms/fm1/` as a **composite community HAL stack** strictly isolated inside `platforms/fm1/` (satisfying `MPL-2.0` file-level scope while keeping `src/` 100% platform-clean):
  1. **Base Shell & `Hold OCT-` Rescue (`zvenson/dinghy` + `hugelton/felucca-bsp`):** Retain `dinghy`'s boot sequence, power-on **`Hold OCT-` USB rescue mode**, non-blocking `LCD_IDLE` `SPI1` DMA strip pipeline, USB/TRS MIDI, and CRC-verified `settings_*` / `slot_*` high-flash partitions for `GLOBAL CONFIG` and the 8 Snapshot slots (`White Keys 9–16`).
  2. **`Core 1` (`cpu1`) Internal SRAM Offload & Uncached Shared Bus (`fm1_cpu1.{h,S}`):** Boot `cpu1` (`charlesvestal/fm1-x0x` / `isod89/sloop-fm1` lineage), link `Core 1`'s `~28–36 KB` hot DSP code + `.rodata` into internal SRAM (`.c1_text`, `< 85 KB` combined with DSP state out of `578 KB` SRAM) to eliminate `cpu0`/`cpu1` XIP cache contention and prevent audio dropouts during `30–100 ms` NOR Flash saves, and place `Core 0 <-> Core 1` shared SPSC/telemetry state in uncached SRAM (or apply explicit D-Cache flush/invalidate).
  3. **Strict IEEE-754 `-mcpu=r3 -mfprev1` Policy & 2-Step NR Reciprocals:** Compile with `-target pi32v2 -mcpu=r3 -mfprev1 -fno-math-errno -ffp-contract=off -fno-exceptions -fno-rtti` (**never `-ffast-math`**), audit symbols for zero `soft-double` / 64-bit integer divisions / unauthorized ROM calls, hoist frame-rate PWM duty reciprocals out of the `4x` loop, and use 2-step Newton-Raphson IEEE-754 reciprocals (`< 1.5e-6` / `~-116 dB` error; never 1-step) where `/` lowers to `__divsf3`.
  4. **16-Bit Headroom Calibration, 1-LSB TPDF Dither & `ALNK0` Crystal Master Clocking:** Pace `Core 1` DSP strictly from the hardware **`ALNK0` I2S `24 MHz` crystal DMA** so the 3.5mm TRS analog output is 100% click- and jitter-free; map the engine's `±1.45f` peak ceiling cleanly inside `[-32768, +32767]` (zero un-oversampled `44.1 kHz` hard clipping); apply **1-LSB TPDF dither** (`~8` cycles/frame) to eliminate 16-bit truncation harmonic fuzz on quiet Vactrol LPG swells into high-gain fuzz pedals; and bridge `±50 ppm` USB-C host clock drift via fractional-sample interpolation on the USB buffer rather than dropping/repeating frames.
  5. **Settled `PA7` / `PA8` `SARADC` Keybed Pressure (`Velo` Technique):** Sample `SARADC` channels 0 & 1 on matrix rows `PA7`/`PA8` (`White Keys 5–16` pressure) with explicit GPIO/mux settling delay so column switching transients never crosstalk into the shared `MASTER` analog pot channel.
  6. **EMI-Safe Multiplexed `74HC595` Varibright Modulation (`5/6-Bit` Gamma / `1st-Order ΔΣ`):** Drive the multiplexed `2 × 74HC595` 42-LED matrix via fixed-rate gamma-mapped PWM or 1st-order delta-sigma dithering rather than raw 8-bit BAM—eliminating `127 <-> 128` BAM flashes, keeping `Core 0` ISR load `< 2%`, and avoiding synchronous `1–4 kHz` ground-plane current spikes into the analog output.
- **Consequences:** Preserves `100%` of Ruido's `4x` oversampled `float32` sound quality and `src/` simulator bit parity, protects the 16-bit DAC and analog 3.5mm line stage against digital clipping, truncation fuzz, USB clock slips, and LED ground whine into external pedals/Eurorack, and gates hardware flashing behind `joakimeriksson/fm1-emu` verification and `Hold OCT-` brick protection.

     - **Left-to-Right Group Gradient, Console `SOLO`/`MUTE` Truth & 42-LED Choreography:** Order rest group brightnesses in a clean left-to-right gradient from brightest to faintest (`64% -> 46% -> 30% -> 18% -> 8%` on top; `64% -> 42% -> 22% -> 8%` on bottom), jumping to `100%` when active; enforce strict console `SOLO`/`MUTE` truth (`SOLO = 100%, MUTE = 0%` when soloed; `MUTE = 100%, SOLO = 0%` whenever silent/muted, with zero side-highlights on `Keys 5–16` or `FX`/`HOME`); choreograph all 42 device LEDs on effect punch-ins and mode swaps; and route every held key 1-to-1 to Ruido's real native visualizations.
- **Consequences:** Frees 100% of the 27-key silicone bed for expressive, non-destructive live performance with zero modal confusion; makes physical keybed zones immediately legible on unlabeled hardware; unifies keyboard performance 100% with Ruido's existing visual language; preserves full DSP alias/headroom safety (`24+x` real-time single-core margin) and scanline strip invariance; and queues Mode 2 (`HARMONIUM`) and Mode 3 (`CIRCUIT LAB`) as self-contained follow-up passes alongside post-playtest evaluation of `GLOBAL` and voice mutes.

## ADR-037: Default `LIVE MIXER` Keyboard, `MONO`/`POLY` Acoustic Unison & Detune Spread, and 4-Slot `128-Byte` Scene Engine (`SAVE` + `Keys 13–16` + `PRESETS`)
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Context:** Playtesting Mode 1 (`DUB CONSOLE`) revealed four concrete UX and acoustic refinements: (1) naming the default performance layout `"DUB CONSOLE"` was misleading on a pure dry drone synthesizer that deliberately omits onboard delay/reverb FX; (2) mapping the `MONO` and `POLY` black keys only to stereo pan narrowing/widening had minimal acoustic impact in stereo and zero effect when `OUTPUT ROUTING` was set to `MONO`; (3) saving scenes with `SAVE` needs to use the exact same physical home as recalling them rather than scattering save/recall across unrelated controls or overloading all 16 keys with slots; and (4) `OCTAVE` (`+1 / +2 Oct`) belongs on the core `LIVE MIXER` layout (`Keys 9–12`), while the rhythmic `STUTTER` chopper fits `3/3 CIRCUIT LAB` (`Keys 9–12`) much more naturally.
- **Decision:**
  1. **Rename Mode 1 (`1/3`) to `LIVE MIXER` & Reorganize Bottom `4 × 4` Zones:** Mode 1 (`1/3`) is now **`LIVE MIXER`** (`MUTE 1–4` on `Keys 1–4`, `FILTER 5–8` on `Keys 5–8`, `OCTAVE 9–12` on `Keys 9–12`, and `SCENE 1–4` on `Keys 13–16` directly below the physical `SAVE` button), while `STUTTER 1–4` moves onto `3/3 CIRCUIT LAB` (`Keys 9–12`).
  2. **High-Impact Acoustic `MONO` (`UNISON`) & `POLY` (`DETUNE SPREAD`) Macros:** `MONO` pulls `OSC 2–4` pitches in log-octave space onto `OSC 1`'s anchor frequency (with tight `~±2 cent` analog VCO unison offsets to avoid sterile digital square phase cancellation), damps `1/f` thermal drift, and centers stereo pan; `POLY` fans `OSC 1–4` into a detuned pitch swarm, shifts per-voice band-limited Schmitt PWM pulse duties (`voicePwDelta`, smoothed clicklessly on Core 1), and widens stereo pan—delivering dramatic timbral impact in both `STEREO` and `MONO` output routing while keeping the live waveform `100%` undimmed.
  3. **4-Slot `128-Byte` CRC-32 Scene Bank (`SAVE` + `Keys 13–16`, `SCENE MODE` & `PRESETS` Encoder):** Store 4 full synthesis patches (`4 × 30 B` quantized log-octave voice records + `circuitCoupling` + header + IEEE CRC-32 = `128 B` exact per slot, keeping total `RuidoController` static RAM `< 2 KB`) pre-populated with 4 musical drone presets. Tapping `SAVE` arms `STORE SCENE` (`Keys 13–16` pulse across any keyboard model) and tapping `Key 13–16` stores into `SCENE 1–4` (swallowing the key release so lifting your finger never re-triggers recall). `GLOBAL CONFIG` adds **`SCENE MODE`** alongside **`MUTE MODE`** and **`SOLO MODE`** using the shared `LATCHING` *(default)* / `MOMENTARY` grammar (`LATCHING` glides smoothly into a scene at pressure-controlled speed or parks at a multi-finger pressure-blended hybrid; `MOMENTARY` morphs non-destructively by finger pressure and restores your bit-exact IEEE-754 float32 home patch on release). Left-hand **`PRESETS`** steps across `SCENE 1–4` across all 3 keyboard models (`0.50 s` Standard, `1.80 s` `OCT-` Fine, `0.10 s` `OCT+` Coarse).
- **Consequences:** Gives drone performers an immediately intuitive default mixer/macro/scene surface where saving and recalling share the exact same 4 pressure-sensitive keys right beneath `SAVE`; uses the full word `SCENE` across every header, column, bracket, and menu with zero abbreviations; makes `MONO` and `POLY` genuinely musical in both stereo and mono rigs; preserves sub-cent pitch accuracy and `< 2 KB` controller RAM budget; and keeps `STUTTER` playable inside `CIRCUIT LAB`.

## ADR-038: 4-Model Carousel (`RHYTHM LAB` Split), Mode 2 (`2/4 CIRCUIT LAB` — `CMOS PATCH BAY`), and Capacitor-Ramp / S&H Patch-Matrix DSP
- **Date:** 2026-11-04 · **Status:** Implemented

- **Decision:**
  1. **Expand `SEL` Carousel to 4 Models & Split Out `4/4 RHYTHM LAB`:** Expand the `SEL` + `SELECT` carousel to **`1/4 LIVE MIXER` -> `2/4 CIRCUIT LAB` -> `3/4 HARMONIUM` -> `4/4 RHYTHM LAB`** (with centered dynamic `N`-bead carousel rendering), moving rhythmic chopping out of `CIRCUIT LAB` into a dedicated `4/4 RHYTHM LAB` model at the end of the carousel.
  2. **Pure CMOS / West-Coast Bottom Row (`MUTE | CROSS-PIN | FOLD | CRUSH`) + Full 42-LED Light Shows on `2/4 CIRCUIT LAB`:** Every column `1–4` bends **that exact oscillator (`OSC 1–4`)** and drives all 42 LEDs across the chassis:
     - **`Keys 5–8` (`CROSS-PIN 1–4`):** Taps neighbor `(v+1)%4`'s raw pre-mute `CD40106` timing-capacitor ramp + self-feedback into **Voice `v` itself** (`±3.2 OCT` FM, comparator XOR edge tear, PWM skew, and `Korg35` resonance—working even when `OSC 1` is soloed), paired with 42-LED high-voltage arc lightning.
     - **`Keys 9–12` (`FOLD 1–4`):** Taps Voice `v`'s internal `CD40106` capacitor triangle ramp into a `4x`-oversampled `1.0x -> 6.2x` sinusoidal Serge wavefolder at locked pitch, paired with symmetrical standing-wave LED crests (`1 -> 6` folds).
     - **`Keys 13–16` (`CRUSH 1–4`):** Cascades a `CD4040` `/2, /4, /8` sub-octave logic XOR pre-filter into a post-`MS-20` `CD4013` zero-order sample-and-hold decimator (`16 kHz -> 165 Hz`) + stepped R-2R rung quantizer, turning all 42 LEDs into a live 16-bit binary ripple counter bus.

- **Consequences:** Makes every bottom-row key on `2/4 CIRCUIT LAB` timbrally transformative on both the live oscilloscope and all 42 hardware LEDs while keeping `1/4 LIVE MIXER` `100%` unchanged, keeping all 57 source files `<= 698/700` lines, preserving `< 48 KB` DSP SRAM and `< -108.5 dBc` alias suppression, and setting up `4/4 RHYTHM LAB` cleanly as its own polyrhythmic gate instrument.

## ADR-040: Mode 3 (`3/3 RHYTHM LAB` — Plain-English Clock Dividers, Euclidean Grooves & Filter Plucks) and Port-Agnostic `24 PPQN` MIDI Clock PLL (`TRS` · `USB-C` · `BLE-MIDI`)
- **Date:** 2026-11-04 · **Status:** Implemented
- **Context:** Designing the third performance keyboard (`3/3 RHYTHM LAB`) surfaced two closely linked requirements: (1) obtuse academic terms ("ratchet", "hocket") hide musical intent, whereas plain-English **free-speed stutter**, **master-clock dividers**, **16-step Euclidean grooves**, **sidechain pump**, **4-voice chase**, **MS-20 filter plucks**, and **shuffle swing** are immediately understandable and reward multi-finger pressure play; and (2) adding clock dividers and Euclidean grooves makes `RHYTHM LAB` the natural moment to introduce external **`24 PPQN` MIDI Clock In**—provided the clock follower is 100% transport-agnostic across **3.5mm TRS cable**, **USB-C**, and **Bluetooth BLE-MIDI** (where `7.5–15 ms` wireless connection intervals clump multiple `0xF8` ticks into a single packet burst).
- **Decision:**
  1. **Plain-English `3/3 RHYTHM LAB` Across All 27 Pressure Keys & 42 LEDs:**
     - **Bottom Row (`MUTE · STUTTER · DIVIDE · EUCLID`):** `Keys 1–4` shared Console Mute; `Keys 5–8` free-running `STUTTER` (`1.5 -> 24 Hz`, independent of master clock for phase-drifting polyrhythms); `Keys 9–12` master-clock `DIVIDE` (`/2, X1, X2, X3, X4, X8`, staggered `/2, X1, X2, X3` across `OSC 1–4` at light touch); and `Keys 13–16` 16-step Bjorklund `EUCLID` (`2/16 .. 16/16` by pressure, rotated `{0, 2, 1, 3}` across `OSC 1–4` so chords interlock, lighting the live 16-step pattern + playhead across `Keys 1–16` while still honoring armed `SAVE` scene storage).
     - **Top Row (`SOLO · PUMP · CLOCK · SWING` + `11th FREEZE`):** `OP1–4` shared Console Solo; `OP5 PUMP` quarter-note/eighth-note sidechain swell; `OP6 CHASE` exclusive `1 -> 2 -> 3 -> 4` round-robin step loop; `PIT TEMPO` `1.0x -> 4.0x` master clock multiplier with live `BPM` / `BPM EXT` readout; `GLO PLUCK` percussive MS-20 low-pass filter plucks paired with the live 4-voice MS-20 passband graph bouncing on screen; `MONO SHORT` `50% -> 6%` pinpoint gate length; `POLY SWING` `50% -> 72%` shuffle warp + stereo ping-pong bounce; and `11th FREEZE` universal stasis lock.
  2. **Transport-Agnostic `24 PPQN` MIDI Clock Follower (`MidiClockTracker`) & `GLOBAL CONFIG` (`MIDI CLOCK: AUTO / OFF`):**

     - Wire WebMIDI (`USB`, `TRS` interfaces, and OS `BLE-MIDI`) immediately in `platforms/sim/web/`, while targeting `dinghy`'s native `TRS` UART + `USB-C` MIDI drivers and JieLi's `AC791N` BLE peripheral GATT service (`13-bit` ms packet timestamp unpacking) in Milestone 4 (`platforms/fm1/`).
  3. **Shared-Coefficient `OnePoleSmootherBank` / `CriticallyDampedSmootherBank` Packing:** Deduplicate identical per-sample smoother coefficients inside Core 1 so adding `RhythmClockState` and `MidiClockTracker` keeps `sizeof(DroneEngine) = 47.94 KB` (`< 48 KB`) and `sizeof(RuidoController) = 2,040 B` (`< 2 KB`).
- **Consequences:** Completes all 3 onboard performance keyboards (`LIVE MIXER`, `CIRCUIT LAB`, `RHYTHM LAB`) in Milestone 3; delivers rock-steady external MIDI clock sync across wired and wireless MIDI transports without loosening SRAM or `700`-line modularity budgets; and documents the exact Milestone 4 hardware driver path for TRS, USB-C, and BLE-MIDI on the FM-1.

## ADR-041: Hardware-Safe Modular Decomposition, Uncached/Cached Core 1 SRAM Split, Anti-Brick Static-Ctor Gate & `dinghy` NVS Contracts
- **Date:** 2026-11-05 · **Status:** Implemented
- **Context:** Preparing the portable codebase for Milestone 4 FM-1 (`zvenson/dinghy` + `AC791N` dual-core `pi32v2`) integration highlighted five modularity and hardware-safety opportunities: (1) several `.cpp` files had grown within `13–25` lines of the `700`-line cap; (2) adding a punch-in modifier required editing 3 parallel `switch` blocks across `core/` and `ui/`; (3) shared Core 0/Core 1 header math risked weak COMDAT deduplication into `0x02000000` XIP NOR Flash (which stalls Core 1 audio during `30–100 ms` `USR` flash erases) or accidental pre-`main` C++ `.init_array` constructors before `dinghy`'s power-on `Hold OCT-` USB rescue check; (4) Core 1 DSP state and inter-core buses lived in a single struct rather than separating uncached IPC SRAM from cached Core 1 DSP SRAM; and (5) Wasm simulator telemetry/summary float arrays relied on raw index literals across C++ and JS.
- **Decision:**
  1. **Single-Source Punch-In Table & Filter Macro Module:** Consolidate all 31 punch-in metadata rows into a single `constexpr` `RODATA` table with zero pointer relocations (blocked from `src/dsp/` by `make check`), extract filter macro math into a dedicated header using a compact POD context struct and division-free `log2` subtraction (`0` `pi32v2` `__divsf3` software divisions), and force-inline cross-core math helpers so Core 1 `.c1_text` SRAM never calls into XIP Flash.
  2. **Cohesive Subsystem Splits (`<= 587 / 700` Lines Everywhere):** Split top-header chrome from bottom-gauge chrome, page navigation from controller orchestration, global column gauge evaluation from global encoder mutation, binary snapshot codec/presets from the snapshot glide bank, and scanline tile/bloom rasterization from oscilloscope trace synthesis.
  3. **Compile-Time Anti-Brick Static-Constructor Gate:** Enforce `-Wglobal-constructors -Wexit-time-destructors` under `-Werror` across native and Wasm builds so portable `src/` can never emit `.init_array` code ahead of `dinghy`'s `Hold OCT-` USB rescue check.
  4. **Cache-Line Isolated `EngineSharedBuses` (`41.9 KB` Uncached) vs Core 1 DSP State (`5.8 KB` Cached) & `dinghy` NVS Ports:** Separate `64 B`-aligned inter-core buses from hot Core 1 ZDF/oscillator/FIR state while preserving the combined wrapper for desktop/Wasm simulators; deduplicate per-target LFO smoother coefficients (`-256 B` net in `DroneEngine`) and narrow LIFO/detent counters (`-72 B` in `RuidoController`); and expose CRC-32 verified `4 x 128 B` slot and `16 B` global-settings binary POD contracts with explicit dirty masks for `dinghy`'s `USR` (`0xEA000–0xFC000`) NVS APIs.
  5. **Verified Wasm Simulator ABI Schema:** Share named telemetry (`48` floats) and summary (`57` floats) index schemas between C++ and JS, enforced automatically by `tools/check_hygiene.py` and `tests/verify_wasm.mjs`.
- **Consequences:** Drops the largest handwritten file in `src/` to `587 / 700` lines (`113+` lines slack), shrinks `DroneEngine` to `48,832 B` and `RuidoController` to `1,968 B`, eliminates Wasm offset drift, and makes Milestone 4 `dinghy` dual-core + NVS wiring a thin, zero-brick-risk adapter layer.

## ADR-042: Logarithmic `ALGORITHM` Master Tempo (`20–3,000 BPM`) & 11th-Key `TAP TEMPO` in `3/3 RHYTHM LAB`
- **Date:** 2026-11-05 · **Status:** Implemented
- **Context:** As an experimental drone and West-Coast rhythm instrument, `3/3 RHYTHM LAB` benefits from spanning slow hypnotic pulses (`20 BPM`) all the way into Moog-Subharmonicon-class audio-rate Euclidean and clock-divided formant modulation (`3,000 BPM` internal, up to `6,000 BPM` with `PIT` `4x` multiplier), while keeping external `24 PPQN` MIDI Clock's `30–300 BPM` BLE/USB burst-rejection PLL window isolated (`Ruido` is MIDI Clock receive-only, so internal `3,000 BPM` never touches `31.25 kbaud` MIDI bandwidth). Because `OCT+` sits directly underneath `ALGORITHM` on the left panel (`~15 mm` apart), `STANDARD` gear must cover `20–3,000 BPM` comfortably one-handed via a logarithmic curve, while remapping `RHYTHM LAB`'s 11th tall key from `FREEZE` to **`TAP TEMPO`** gives performers immediate live beat-matching and downbeat phase alignment.
- **Decision:**
  1. **3-Gear Logarithmic `ALGORITHM` Tempo (`20–3,000 BPM`):** In `3/3 RHYTHM LAB` (when `GLOBAL CONFIG` is closed), `ALGORITHM` sweeps internal Master Tempo logarithmically across `20.0–3,000.0 BPM` (`2.0` octaves/turn = `1` semitone per 4-tick detent in `STANDARD`, `~0.2 BPM`/detent in `Hold OCT-` `FINE`, `6.0` octaves/turn in `Hold OCT+` `COARSE`), persisted in `0.1 BPM` resolution (`200..30000`) inside the existing 16-byte CRC-32 device settings payload. Above `300 BPM`, Core 1 adaptively tightens vactrol gate smoothing (`2.2 ms -> 0.35 ms`) so audio-rate Euclidean/divided gates stay crisp and clickless, while `60 Hz` keybed LEDs smoothly damp fast step cursors above `320 BPM` to prevent visual strobing.
  2. **11th Tall Key = `TAP TEMPO` in `3/3 RHYTHM LAB` (`FREEZE` Retained in `LIVE MIXER` & `CIRCUIT LAB`):** Tapping the rightmost tall raised key in `RHYTHM LAB` aligns Beat 1 immediately on the first tap (`3.2 s` sequence reset, `40 ms` switch-bounce guard) and averages up to 3 consecutive quarter-note tap intervals (`1.75x` tempo-jump auto-reset) into internal BPM (or phase-aligns Beat 1 without breaking lock when externally MIDI-synced), pulsing its tall LED slit gently on the quarter-note beat at rest.
  3. **Log-Space HUD Gauge, `SEL` `3/3` Discovery & Explicit `MIDI * IN` Global Settings:** Both `TEMPO` (`ALGORITHM`) and `TAP TEMPO` share a unified log-octave header bar gauge (remaining steady across consecutive taps via a `1.5 s` release toast), `SEL` `3/3 RHYTHM LAB` displays live `ALGORITHM / TAP` (or `MIDI CLOCK`) BPM + a `TAP` bracket over the 11th key (while the full `SEL` illustration + pressure-sensitivity guidance slowly dim to `50%` after `5 s` idle and wake back to `100%` on `SELECT` / `ALGORITHM` turns), and `GLOBAL CONFIG` exposes explicit input-labeled **`MIDI CLOCK IN`** (`AUTO` / `OFF`), **`MIDI TRANSPORT IN`** (`OFF` default / `PHASE` / `RUN/STOP`), and **`MIDI CHANNEL IN`** (`OMNI` / `CH 1–16`) inside the existing 16-byte NVS settings layout.
- **Consequences:** Expands `RHYTHM LAB` from traditional grooves into pitched audio-rate polyrhythmic synthesis with one-handed Semitone-per-detent control, adds instant downbeat-aligned Tap Tempo on the 11th key, lets DAWs/grooveboxes optionally gate `PLAY/STOP` via MIDI transport, filters incoming channelized MIDI cleanly in multi-synth setups, and keeps the 16-byte NVS settings layout unchanged.

## ADR-044: FM-1 Gate 4.1 Toolchain Audit — Native `r3` Hardware `fdiv`, Custom `pi32v2` Lock-Free Atomics, `GPL-3.0-only` `dinghy` Boundary, and Scalar ZDF Profile
- **Date:** 2026-11-05 · **Status:** Implemented (refines license & `__divsf3` assumptions in ADR-034)
- **Context:** Bringing up the reproducible `linux/amd64` JieLi `pi32v2` Clang 4.0.1 toolchain (`tools/fm1-docker/Dockerfile`), `zvenson/dinghy`, and `joakimeriksson/fm1-emu` (`make fm1-check`) surfaced four concrete hardware, compiler, and licensing findings that supersede earlier estimates:
  1. **Upstream `dinghy` Licensing (`GPL-3.0-only`, Not `MPL-2.0`):** Auditing upstream `LICENSING.md` confirmed `zvenson/dinghy` (`platform.c` + `hal/` + `tools/`) is licensed **`GPL-3.0-only`** (whereas JieLi's 3 `AC79` SDK boot blobs are `Apache-2.0` and `fm1-emu` is `MIT`; `dinghy`'s non-GPL upstream logo assets are stripped at image build).
  2. **100% Native `r3` Hardware `fdiv` (`0` `__divsf3` Calls):** Disassembly of the linked `pi32v2` ELF (`-mcpu=r3 -mfprev1`) proved that `AC791N` silicon lowers single-precision `a / b` directly to a `2.66`-cycle hardware `r = r / r (f)` instruction (`0` software `__divsf3` calls across the entire firmware), making software Newton-Raphson division workarounds slower and unnecessary.
  3. **JieLi `libcompiler_rt` `__sync_*` IRQ Clobber & `1-Byte` `libc` Bottlenecks:** Default Clang 4.0.1 `std::atomic` lowering linked `libcompiler_rt`'s `__sync_lock_test_and_set_4` / `__sync_val_compare_and_swap_4`, whose `rep 8 2 { r3 = icfg; cli }` prologue cleared CPU interrupt enable (`icfg` bit 9) inside `fm1-emu` (halting `ALNK0` audio and `TIMER5` ticks) while hammering `cli` + global inter-core `lockset` spinlocks 44 times per audio block. Meanwhile, `dinghy`'s 1-byte `memset`/`memcpy` and 4-wide SIMD SoA filter arrays spilled 567 stack operations on scalar 16-GPR `pi32v2`.
  4. **Honest `fm1-emu` Instruction Budget Entering Gate 4.2:** Calibrated `fm1-emu` cycle benchmarks (counting VLIW pairs sequentially on a single core) measure **`~9,693` insns/frame** idle (`-37%` from `15,296`) and **`~18,448` insns/frame** under full 4-voice nonlinear `HPF+LPF` self-oscillation + overdrive + `8x` LFO stress vs `5,442` (`240 MHz`) / `7,256` (`320 MHz`) single-core cycles/frame.
- **Decision:**
  1. **Externalized Docker BSP & Explicit `GPL-3.0-only` Notice:** Keep `zvenson/dinghy` strictly inside the reproducible Docker build image (`/opt/dinghy`) rather than vendoring third-party code into Ruido's Git repository; note explicitly that any distributed `.fwsc` binary linked against `dinghy`'s `platform.c` inherits `GPL-3.0-only` source-disclosure obligations unless linked against an `MPL-2.0`-only `felucca-bsp` shell.
  2. **Freestanding `pi32v2` `std::atomic`, 32-Bit Word `memset`/`memcpy` & Build-Time Symbol Gates:** Provide a dedicated `platforms/fm1/libc/` freestanding C++ header set implementing aligned volatile load/store + `ssync` fences for acquire/release (`0` `cli`, `0` `lockset`) and single-pass `icfg` save + `cli` + `lockset` for CAS, replace 1-byte `memset`/`memcpy` with 32-bit word-unrolled routines in `platforms/fm1/`, and fail `make fm1-check` automatically if any `__sync_*`, `soft-double`, `64-bit` integer division, `.init_array` static constructor, or chip-ROM symbol links.
  3. **Scalar Register-Resident ZDF Dispatch & Idle Fast-Paths (`0.000e+00` Bit Parity):** Dispatch per-voice register-resident MS-20 ZDF filtering on non-SIMD scalar targets (`-6.9 KiB` Core 1 `.text`, `46.5 KiB` total), bypass inactive LFO/rhythm/circuit modulation branches, and refresh `prepareCoeffs` at `11.03 kHz` (`4`-sample sub-chunks) with zero regression across all 17 `make test-dsp` acoustic gates (`< -108.65 dBc` alias floor).
  4. **Gate 4.2 Dual-Core + `320 MHz` Plan:** Target `320 MHz` PLL + `.c1_text` zero-wait-state SRAM + sub-block (`8–16` sample) control-rate LFO/smoother interpolation (and dual-core voice/modulation split if required by silicon profiling) in Gate 4.2.
- **Consequences:** Completes Gate 4.1 with an automated one-command `make fm1-check` pipeline: `ruido.bin = 234.4 KB` (`40.3%` of `567.9 KiB` `APP_SLOT`), `ruido.fwsc = 597.9 KiB` (`< 600 KiB`), `.data+.bss = 11.8 KiB / 96 KiB`, `.pool = 165.1 KiB / 336 KiB`, `59` rendered audio blocks + live scanline LCD frames in `fm1-emu` normal boot, and verified power-on `Hold OCT-` USB rescue isolation before any C++ constructor executes.

## ADR-045: FM-1 Gate 4.2 Dual-Core `.c1_text` SRAM DSP, Dekker `2+2` ZDF Work-Stealing, Silicon-Lottery Fault Survival, and `ALNK0`-Paced TPDF + USB Drift Audio
- **Date:** 2026-11-05 · **Status:** Implemented
- **Context:** Entering Gate 4.2, single-core execution required `~9,693` insns/frame idle and `~18,448` insns/frame under full 4-voice simultaneous `HPF+LPF` Korg35 self-oscillation + overdrive + `8x` LFO modulation against `5,442` (`240 MHz`) and `7,256` (`320 MHz`) cycles/frame. In addition, running `Core 1` (`cpu1`) reliably on physical `AC791N` silicon and `joakimeriksson/fm1-emu` requires: (1) zero XIP SPI NOR Flash instruction or `.rodata` fetches from `Core 1` (preventing `30–100 ms` sector-erase stalls during `USR` saves), (2) zero `idle` instructions on `Core 1` (due to shared clock-gate hazard on `AC791N`) and zero hardware `lockset` spinlocks, (3) graceful survival on silicon-lottery units exhibiting `Core 1` bit-21 (`0x00200000`) SRAM data-load glitches (`--flaky-core1`), and (4) clean 16-bit DAC quantization and USB-C UAC1 host clock-drift bridging while keeping all new hardware/DSP primitives isolated in first-party modules so external `dinghy` remains cleanly replaceable.
- **Decision:**
  1. **Zero-Wait-State `.c1_text` SRAM Execution (`0x01C0E000..0x01C20000`, `64.5 KiB`, `0` XIP Refs):** Relocate 100% of `Core 1` DSP `.text`, `.rodata`, weak inline helpers, and dedicated word-aligned `ruido_c1_memcpy`/`ruido_c1_memset` into internal SRAM (`ruido_fm1.ld` + ELF section/symbol rewriter in `platforms/fm1/build_fm1.py`), booting `Core 1` via clean-room `fm1_cpu1.{h,c,S}` strictly *after* power-on `Hold OCT-` USB rescue clears and enforcing `0` XIP (`0x02000000+`) references, `0` `idle`, and `0` `lockset` at build time.
  2. **16-Frame Span Stepping, `[r++]`/`[r--]` FIR Walks & Zero-Wait Dekker `2+2` ZDF Work-Stealing (`VoiceFilterCoProcessor`):** Amortize control smoothers, 8-LFO stepping, oscillator drift/PWM setup (`SchmittOscillatorBank::processSpan4x`), MS-20 coefficient preparation (`Ms20DualFilter::processSpan4x`), signed `int32` `itof`/`ftoi` phase casts, `[r++]`/`[r--]` polyphase FIR pointer walks, and batched `ScopeRingBuffer` memory barriers across `16`-sample (`2.76 kHz`) spans with linearly interpolated per-sample audio-rate pitch/PWM/gain ramps (`< -108.65 dBc` alias rejection preserved). Pair `Core 1` (`Voices 0–1`) with a 100% CAS-free Dekker work-stealing SPSC co-processor (`VoiceFilterCoProcessor`, `0.0e+00` float32 parity verified under TSan): `Core 0` opportunistically assists `Voices 2–3` between 4-row LCD scanline slices, and if `Core 0` is busy when `Core 1` finishes `Voices 0–1`, `Core 1` reclaims `Voices 2–3` in `0` wait cycles (`0` DMA underruns). Also upgrade `LockFreeControlBus` from CAS to a 3-slot CAS-free Dekker handoff.
  3. **3-Layer Silicon-Lottery `Core 1` Fault Isolation (`--flaky-core1` Survival):** Map `Core 1`'s `AC791N` interrupt vector bank (`0x01EEF300`, `+0x200`) to fault handlers, run a stackless register-only `512`-read SRAM canary POST (`ruido_fm1_c1_boot_post`, verified `0` `[sp` instructions) before any C++ stack frame plus per-block canary checks, and backstop with `Core 0` DMA stall detection + hardware `C1_CON` (`0x01EEE004`) pause so affected `AC791N` units automatically switch to glitch-free solo `Core 0` execution without halting audio or UI.
  4. **`ALNK0`-Paced Pre-Dither `MASTER` Scaling, `±1.45f` 1-LSB TPDF Quantizer & `±50 ppm` USB Drift Resampler (`fm1_audio_io.h`):** Pace `Core 1` strictly from `ALNK0` `24 MHz` crystal `256`-frame DMA consumption via an 8-block (`512`-frame) cache-line-isolated `Fm1AudioBlockRing` (`Core 0` DMA ISR = `~8.8` insns/frame), smooth the `MASTER` pot in float32 *before* stereo uncorrelated **1-LSB TPDF triangular dither** (`22595.0f` scale mapping `±1.45f` cleanly inside `[-32768, +32767]`), and bridge `±50 ppm` `44.1 / 48 kHz` USB-C UAC1 host clock drift via a PI-servoed 4-point Hermite stereo interpolator (`Fm1UsbDriftResampler`).
- **Consequences:** Cuts `fm1-emu` Dual-Core Idle cost to **`~4,523` insns/frame** (`83.1%` @ `240 MHz`, `62.3%` @ `320 MHz`) and full 4-voice Korg35 ZDF + overdrive + `8x` LFO Stress cost to **`~6,976` insns/frame** (`96.1%` @ `320 MHz`, **`-62%`** vs Gate 4.1) with **`0` underruns**, `ruido.fwsc = 597.9 KiB` (`< 600 KiB`), `.c1_text = 64.5 KiB / 72 KiB`, `.data+.bss = 11.9 KiB`, `.pool = 168.6 KiB`, verified `--flaky-core1 0.05` `Core 0` survival, and verified power-on `Hold OCT-` USB rescue isolation.

## ADR-046: FM-1 Gate 4.3 Hardware I/O — Cross-Frame `30 MHz` Scanline DMA, Settled `PA7`/`PA8` Pressure, `909.1 Hz` Multi-Tier `ΔΣ` LEDs, Bare-Metal Wired MIDI Boundary, Cost-Balanced `1+3`/`2+2` ZDF Delegation & Dual-JEDEC `USR` NVS
- **Date:** 2026-11-06 · **Status:** Implemented (refines Milestone 4 BLE-MIDI timing in ADR-034/ADR-040)
- **Context:** Completing FM-1 firmware hardware integration (Gate 4.3) surfaced five concrete engineering decisions on physical `AC791N` (`pi32v2`) constraints, analog noise-floor hygiene, and firmware bloat:
  1. **JieLi BLE-MIDI Stack Bloat vs Bare-Metal `v1.0` Purity:** Unlike `3.5mm TRS` UART1 DMA (`PH8`) and `USB-C` MIDI—which run in a few hundred bytes of clean-room C—onboard `AC791N` Bluetooth LE requires linking proprietary JieLi `libbtctrler` / `libbtstack` RTOS binary blobs (`~180–240 KB` Flash + radio IRQ/SRAM overhead), conflicting with our `< 600 KiB` `.fwsc` budget, deterministic bare-metal dual-core execution, and zero-blob auditability.
  2. **Analog Ground-Bounce Hygiene on `2 × 74HC595` (`42` LEDs):** Naive binary-angle or `1–4 kHz` group PWM across 42 LEDs injects audible rail buzz into `+40 dB` external distortion/reverb pedals and creates mid-scale transitions glitches; meanwhile, the FM-1 wires `PLAY` (Green, `col 8, row 1`) and `STOP` (Red/Amber, `col 10, row 2`) as two independent matrix intersections.
  3. **Settled `PA7`/`PA8` Carbon-Pill `SARADC` Multiplexing:** `PA7`/`PA8` (`SARADC` ch `0`/`1`) share the `AC791N` ADC converter with the `PB6` `MASTER` pot (`ch 4`) across matrix columns `2..9` (`F3..C5`, `20` keys) and require single-column isolation + RC settling before conversion, plus a clean digital-pressure fallback when running inside `fm1-emu` (which leaves key-row `SARADC` unmodeled at open-circuit `1023`).
  4. **Asymmetric Single-Voice Nonlinear ZDF Load Under Full-Screen HUD Overlays:** Pressing a single per-oscillator punch-in (`OSC 1` `STUTTER` / `FILTER`) while a full-screen vector HUD (`SEL` keybed illustration) is open concentrates two nonlinear Newton-Raphson ZDF stages onto one voice while `Core 0` is simultaneously compositing scanlines.
  5. **Autumn 2026 `UC25HQ80` (`0xB36014`) SPI NOR Flash Compatibility & Glitch-Free `USR` Saves:** Newer FM-1 production batches populate `UC25HQ80` (`0xB36014`) instead of Puya `P25Q80` (`0x856014`); furthermore, `4 KiB` SPI NOR sector erases mask `Core 0` interrupts for `~35 ms`, which would loop `ALNK0` DMA audio if not silenced beforehand.
- **Decision:**
  1. **100% Bare-Metal Wired MIDI for `v1.0`; Onboard `AC791N` BLE-MIDI GATT Moved to `v1.x+`:** Route `3.5mm TRS` (`PH8` `31.25 kbaud` UART1 circular DMA) and `USB-C` MIDI System Real-Time bytes (`0xF8/0xFA/0xFB/0xFC`) directly into the `24 PPQN` PLL stamped against the hardware `24 MHz` `TIMER4` counter (`41.67 ns` resolution, polled both in `app_idle` and between 16-row LCD strips), keeping `0` JieLi BLE/RTOS binary blobs in `v1.0` and tracking onboard `AC791N` BLE-MIDI GATT (`03B80E5A-...`) + `BLE MIDI` radio toggle in Post-Launch (`v1.x+`).
  2. **Persistent Cross-Frame `30 MHz` (`LCD_BAUD=1`) `SPI1` Scanline DMA & 2-Row `Core 0` DSP Assist:** Stream `240x16` big-endian RGB565 strips across two persistent ping-pong buffers without end-of-frame stalls, polling `Core 0` ZDF DSP assist every 2 scanlines (bypassing sub-slicing automatically if `Core 1` silicon-lottery fallback is active).
  3. **Round-Robin Settled `PA7`/`PA8` `SARADC` Scanner & Pre-Dither `PB6` `MASTER` Pot:** Settle `PB6` `MASTER` pot IIR state at boot before constructing C++ state, route live `MASTER` changes into float32 master trim ahead of 1-LSB TPDF dither, and scan held `F3..C5` keys round-robin inside `TIMER5`'s post-column-deselect window (`~12 us` settle at `24 MHz` ADC clock, `730 -> 140` raw conductance curve, `0.90` digital fallback when `raw >= 1008`).
  4. **EMI-Safe `909.1 Hz` Staggered-Phase Multi-Tier `ΔΣ` 42-LED Modulator:** Map perceptual `2.2`-gamma brightness across 42 channels (splitting `PLAY/STOP` cleanly onto Green `(8, 1)` when running and Red `(10, 2)` when stopped) through Golden-Ratio phase-staggered 1st-order error-diffusion modulators choosing per frame between `0`, `2 us` (`fm1_led_bg`), `4 us` (`fm1_led_dim`), and `95 us` (`fm1_led`) pulses (`0` BAM mid-scale glitches, ultrasonic-spread current transitions).
  5. **Cost-Balanced `1+3` / `2+2` Dekker ZDF Delegation & 16-Block Audio Ring:** Dynamically partition the 4 MS-20 ZDF voice chains by active nonlinearity (`1` nonlinear voice on `Core 1` + `3` linear voices offered to `Core 0`, or heaviest+lightest `2+2` pairing when multiple voices go nonlinear, `0.0e+00` float32 parity), hoist per-frame ZDF denormal scrubs and lazy active-half-cycle PWM increments, and size the `ALNK0` SPSC ring to `16 x 64 = 1,024` frames (`~17.4 ms` post-pop cushion).
  6. **Dual-CRC `0xF4000–0xF8FFF` `USR` Flash NVS & Dual-JEDEC Enforcement:** Store Scene slots `1–4` in `4 KiB` sectors `0xF4000..0xF7FFF` (`128 B` inner CRC-32 + outer `STORE_MAGIC` `"RUID"` CRC-32) and `GLOBAL CONFIG` in `0xF8000` (`16 B`, `1.5 s` idle-key debounce; immediate on `SAVE + Key 13–16`), zeroing `ALNK0` DMA buffers ahead of sector erase and enforcing both `P25Q80` (`0x856014`) and `UC25HQ80` (`0xB36014`) JEDEC IDs across `loader.bin` and `ruido.bin` in `make fm1-check`.
- **Consequences:** Completes Gate 4.3 with all 5 `fm1-emu` hardware verification suites passing (`0` audio underruns across timed `SPI1` DMA + `SEL` overlay + `OSC 1` nonlinear ZDF + `100 BPM` `24 PPQN` MIDI sync; verified `0xF4000–0xF8FFF` SPI0 write-trace + cold power-on Scene/Settings restore; `--flaky-core1` survival; `Hold OCT-` USB rescue), `ruido.bin = 295.7 KB` (`50.8%` of `APP_SLOT`), `ruido.fwsc = 597.9 KiB` (`< 600 KiB`), `.c1_text = 65.5 KiB / 72 KiB`, Dual-Core Idle **`~4,255` insns/frame** (`58.6%` @ `320 MHz`), and Dual-Core Full Stress **`~6,666` insns/frame** (`91.9%` @ `320 MHz`).

## ADR-051: User-Selectable `KEY SENSITIVITY` Touch Curve in `GLOBAL CONFIG`
- **Date:** 2026-11-11 · **Status:** Implemented (extends ADR-050 carbon-pill calibration)
- **Context:** On-device tuning of `FM-1_960` produced a calibrated `[528 .. 815]` carbon-pill curve that feels right with a typical finger, but preferred touch force differs between players.
- **Decision:** Add a `KEY SENSITIVITY` row to `GLOBAL CONFIG` (before `FACTORY RESET`) with five presets — `VERY HIGH`, `HIGH`, **`MEDIUM`** (default = the ADR-050 calibrated curve), `LOW`, `VERY LOW`. Lighter presets move the full-pressure point to higher ADC readings (less force) with a more linear taper; firmer presets push it toward the physical PCB series-resistance floor (never below it, so `100%` stays reachable) with a steeper cubic taper. The setting persists in the existing 16-byte `DeviceSettingsBlob` (formerly reserved byte), where `0` decodes as `MEDIUM` so legacy settings keep the calibrated default without a schema bump.
- **Consequences:** Players can trade touch lightness against dynamic resolution without firmware changes; `MEDIUM` behavior is bit-identical to the previous release, and the NVS layout and CRC scheme stay unchanged.

## ADR-052: Erase-Free `0xF8020..0xF8FF0` Append-Only `DeviceSettingsBlob` Journal & Deduplication
- **Date:** 2026-11-14 · **Status:** Implemented
- **Context:** On physical `FM-1_960` hardware, continuous drone audio and the `10 kHz` `TIMER5` LED matrix scan never stop during live play. Saving `DeviceSettingsBlob` (`16 B`) `1.5 s` after panel release previously erased the full `4 KiB` SPI NOR sector at `0xF8000` (`~45–60 ms` with CPU0 interrupts masked via `cli`) and zeroed the `ALNK0` DMA buffer—causing a ~50 ms audio cutout synchronized with a ~50 ms keyboard LED blackout whenever playing pressure keys in `LIVE MIXER` switched active `SCENE 1–4` slots (or after changing `SEL` model, `TEMPO`, or `GLOBAL CONFIG`).
- **Decision:**
  1. **Deduplicate Unchanged Settings:** Skip live settings writes entirely when the exported `DeviceSettingsBlob` matches the last persisted settings (e.g., switching `Scene 1 -> Scene 2 -> Scene 1` before the `1.5 s` debounce expires).
  2. **Append-Only `16 B` Tail Journal (`0xF8020..0xF8FF0`, `254` Slots):** During live play, append CRC-32-verified `16 B` `DeviceSettingsBlob` records sequentially into the erased `0xFF` tail of sector `0xF8000` (`~150 µs` single-page SPI NOR program, zero sector erase, zero `ALNK0` DMA muting, zero `TIMER5` LED interruption).
  3. **Cold-Boot Scan & Silent Pre-Audio Compaction:** On cold boot (before Core 1, `ALNK0` audio, or LEDs start), scan `0xF8000` + `0xF8020..0xF8FF0` to restore the latest valid CRC-32 record (skipping any torn power-loss slot), and silently compact `0xF8000` back to `0` journal entries whenever `>= 64` slots have accumulated (or on explicit `SAVE + Scene`).
- **Consequences:** Eliminates live background audio dropouts and synchronized LED flashes across all modes while preserving `1.5 s` power-off persistence and reducing `0xF8000` flash sector wear by `64x–254x`.

## ADR-053: One-Handed Multi-Press Page Cycling, `HOME` Oscillator Core (`FREQUENCY · DUTY · DRIFT`) vs. `EDIT` Console Mixer (`LEVEL · PAN`), `LFO A1/B1..A4/B4` Naming & True-Zero `0%` Volume Silence
- **Date:** 2026-11-14 · **Status:** Implemented (updates right-grid button cycling & LFO naming in ADR-028/ADR-029/ADR-036)
- **Context:** Hands-on `FM-1_960` play surfaced four UX and calibration refinements: (1) switching sub-pages inside `FX`, `SEL`, `EDIT`, and `LFO` required reaching across to left-hand `SELECT`; (2) housing raw oscillator `DUTY` and `DRIFT` inside `EDIT` alongside mixer `LEVEL` and `PAN` forced 4-tap mixer cycling while `ENV` must remain free for future envelopes; (3) naming LFOs `LFO A1/B1..A4/B4` (retaining the dimmed `OSC 1..4` subtext and pairing `A1..A4 RATE/DEPTH` cross-mod labels) makes per-oscillator LFO identity even clearer; and (4) `MASTER` or `LEVEL` at `"0%"` still leaked faint audio due to pot CCW wiper resistance, ADC smoothing deadband trapping, `(0, 0.5%)` label rounding, and unconditional 1-LSB TPDF DAC dither.
- **Decision:**
  1. **`HOME` = Clean-Scope Escape + 4-Step Oscillator Core (`Clean Scope -> FREQUENCY -> DUTY -> DRIFT -> Clean Scope`), `EDIT` = Pure 2-Mode Console Mixer (`LEVEL <-> PAN`), `ENV` Reserved:** Pressing `HOME` while any other page/hold/toast is active dismisses in 1 tap (`150 ms` fade-out) to the clean scope; pressing `HOME` from the clean scope cycles `FREQUENCY -> DUTY -> DRIFT -> Clean Scope` (`SELECT` also steps `0..2` while `HOME` is open, keeping the live oscilloscope trace visible behind translucent `DUTY` and `DRIFT` reticles). `EDIT` becomes a fast 2-mode mixer toggle (`LEVEL <-> PAN`, remembering `PAN` on close), keeping `ENV` unassigned for future envelopes.
  2. **Multi-Press Sub-Page Cycling on `FX`, `SEL`, `EDIT`, `LFO` + `LFO A1/B1..A4/B4` Naming:** Tapping an unopened right-grid switch opens its remembered sub-page (`SEL` opens on the active keyboard model without changing it), and subsequent taps cycle continuously—**`FX`** (`OSC 1..4`), **`SEL`** (`LIVE MIXER -> CIRCUIT LAB -> RHYTHM LAB`), **`EDIT`** (`LEVEL <-> PAN`), and **`LFO`** (**`LFO A1 -> LFO B1 -> LFO A2 -> LFO B2 -> LFO A3 -> LFO B3 -> LFO A4 -> LFO B4`**, with voice-specific **`A1..A4 RATE`** and **`A1..A4 DEPTH`** cross-mod labels on `KNOB 1`)—while **`SELECT`** continues stepping in parallel.
  3. **Bit-Exact `0%` Volume Silence:** Calibrate a clean CCW wiper floor on the `MASTER` pot (`Q12 <= 36` mapped to exact zero with continuous `(36..4088]` rescale and zero-endpoint passthrough past the pot jitter deadband), clamp master and per-voice channel gain at `<= 0.01%` to exact zero while rounding `(0.01%, 0.5%)` up to `"1%"` so `"0%"` always means true silence, and bypass 1-LSB TPDF dither on settled silent Core 1 blocks (`0x0000` PCM samples).
- **Consequences:** Separates raw oscillator voice design (`HOME`) cleanly from console mixing (`EDIT`) while reserving `ENV` for future envelopes, makes per-oscillator LFO ownership unmistakable at a glance (`LFO A1/B1..A4/B4`), enables complete one-handed right-side page + parameter control, and guarantees dead-silent `0x0000` DAC output whenever `MASTER` or `LEVEL` reads `0%`.

## ADR-054: Endless `360°` Circular Scene Morph, Self-Discoverable 2-Step `SAVE`/`DELETE` Keybed Flow, Home Base Snap-Back & Erase-Free Scene/Live NVS Journals
- **Date:** 2026-11-14 · **Status:** Implemented (supersedes Snapshot UX & bottom `KNOB 1–4` mix bar in ADR-025/ADR-027/ADR-038/ADR-047; extends ADR-052 NVS journal architecture)
- **Context:** Real-world performance testing exposed four friction points in the earlier snapshot system: (1) linear bounded sweeps on `PRESETS` (`1 <-> 4` with `0.5`/`0.7` 3-scene blend overlap and `KNOB 1–4` snapshot weights displayed in the bottom strip) stopped at hard ends, leaked adjacent third scenes near a slot, and visually conflated `PRESETS`/`SAVE` with `KNOB 1–4`; (2) `SAVE` -> `SELECT` -> `DELETE SCENE` hid scene deletion behind an unprompted encoder turn; (3) `LATCHING` `SCENE 1–4` keys had no fast way back to the active scene after tweaking knobs, nor clean chord-release preservation when lifting multi-finger scene blends, and tweaked live state vanished across power cycles; and (4) `SAVE`/`DELETE` on `0xF4000..0xF7FFF` still erased full `4 KiB` SPI NOR sectors (`~50 ms` audio/LED dropout).
- **Decision:**
  1. **Endless `360°` Circular `PRESETS` Loop & Open 4-Node Track:** Wrap `PRESETS` circularly across occupied scenes (`1 ── 2 ── 3 ── 4 ── 1`, automatically skipping `EMPTY` slots) with pairwise smoothstep crossfading and a `±2`-tick (`0.088 > 1/12` detent) `100%` node plateau (`OCT-` `FINE`, `OCT+` `COARSE` discrete circular scene step). Turning `PRESETS` from an `EDITED` live drone captures the current live synthesis state and smoothly crossfades onto the circular loop over `6` detents. Both `PRESETS` and `LIVE MIXER` `Keys 9–12` suppress bottom `KNOB 1–4` labels completely and render an open, container-free **4-Node Circular Loop Track** (`48 px` equal spans inset from `KNOB 1–4` columns) over a soft top gradient scrim.
  2. **Self-Discoverable 2-Step `SAVE` Manager (`SCENE 1/2` `STORE SCENE >` <-> `SCENE 2/2` `< DELETE SCENE` -> Exit):** Repeated presses of `SAVE` step cleanly through `SCENE 1/2` `STORE SCENE >` (flashing all 4 saveable `Keys 9–12` `25%–95%` at `2.50 Hz` in sync with `SAVE`, paired with open container-free 4-slot status columns, `SEL`-identical 27-key FM-1 diagram + `"SCENE"` bracket, and calm 2-line footer) -> `SCENE 2/2` `< DELETE SCENE` (flashing occupied deletable slots + `SAVE` twice as fast at `5.00 Hz`, `0%` dark safe no-op on `EMPTY`) -> clean `150 ms` exit on the 3rd press (`HOME` or turning synthesis knobs `KNOB 1–4` / `ALGORITHM` exits immediately; `SELECT` toggles `1/2 <-> 2/2` in parallel; storing or deleting dissolves cleanly to header-only `SAVED` / `DELETED` confirmations).
  3. **`EDITED` Hardware Cues, 1-Tap Home Base Snap-Back, Multi-Finger Chord Freeze & `LIVE` Power-Off Persistence:** When tweaked away from a stored scene, the Home Base scene key drops from `100%` to `50%` steady brightness while the `SAVE` LED breathes gently at `12%–28%` (`0%` off when sitting on a pure stored scene, synced 1-to-1 at `2.50 Hz` with the on-screen diagram when armed). Tapping the `50%` Home Base key in `LATCHING` mode smoothly glides back to `100%` of that stored scene; holding `2–4` scene keys sculpts pressure weights continuously and holds the sculpted hybrid steady across asynchronous finger lifts until all fingers release; and idle `1.5 s` auto-save persists unsaved `EDITED` live audio alongside the Home Base index.
  4. **Erase-Free `128 B` Scene & `LIVE` Scratchpad NVS Journals (`0xF4000..0xF9FFF`):** Extend `ADR-052`'s append-only CRC-32 journaling across sectors `0..3` + `5` (`30` `128 B` slots/sector = `120` erase-free live Scene stores/deletes per session + `0xF9000` `LIVE` scratchpad at `~500 µs` and `0` live `4 KiB` erases), stopping cold-boot blank-flash scans after two consecutive empty records and compacting accumulated tail logs before audio startup.
- **Consequences:** Makes `PRESETS` a true endless circular performance control, surfaces `STORE` and `DELETE` transparently on both the screen and 27-key LEDs without hidden chords or `KNOB 1–4` clutter, preserves spontaneous `EDITED` drones across power cycles with instant 1-tap Home Base recovery, and eliminates every live flash-erase audio/LED dropout on FM-1 hardware (`< 2 KiB` controller RAM budget).

## ADR-055: Hardware-Exact `20 + 7` Keybed Architecture, Calibrated LED Grammar, Single-State `MUTE`/`SHIFT + SOLO`, Direct `SHIFT` Keyboard Select & Clean Two-Bay `SEL` Explorer
- **Date:** 2026-11-16 · **Status:** Implemented (supersedes `PIT/GLO` binary `SOLO`, `FREEZE` `11th` key, dual-latch `SOLO MODE`, model-dependent `ALGORITHM`, and `SWING` stereo coupling in ADR-036/ADR-048/ADR-050)
- **Context:** Auditing the FM-1's physical `74HC595` × `PA6/PA7/PA8` matrix alongside live hardware playtesting surfaced six UX and ergonomic refinements: (1) `PIT` and `GLO` sit on `PA8` (`SARADC` Ch 1) and are true 10-bit analog carbon-pill pressure keys—only the rightmost 7 keys (`MONO`, `POLY`, `11th`, and `Keys 13–16` on `PA6`) are digital-only switches; (2) `TAP TEMPO` (`POLY`) had no clock meaning in `1/3 LIVE MIXER` or `2/3 CIRCUIT LAB`, while `SHIFT` needed an unmistakable `100%` beacon and `HOME` needed to stay `100%` lit across all Home screens (including Clean Scope with zero UI); (3) subtle `16–22%` / `28%` physical PWM differences vanished under the FM-1's gamma curve, calling for a strict `0% / 15% / 50% / 100%` functional LED grammar plus calibrated `65% → 40% → 15%` pressure group zones; (4) separate latching `MUTE` and `SOLO` states sharing `Keys 13–16` created conflicting hidden states, whereas a single mixer state with pre-Solo mix memory covers both instant unmuting and 1-tap mix recall cleanly while steady `100% / 50% / 0%` voice-output LEDs show audible (`>= 20 Hz`), sub-audio (`< 20 Hz`), and silent/muted voices at a glance; (5) pressure pads and switches looked identical on screen and `SEL`/`SHIFT` carried verbose prose; and (6) `RHYTHM LAB` `"SWING"` conflated off-beat timing warp with stereo pan bounce.
- **Decision:**
  1. **Hardware-Exact `20 + 7` Keybed Split & Calibrated LED Grammar (`0% / 15% / 50% / 80% / 100%` + `65% → 40% → 15%` Zones):** Dedicate all **20 `PA8`/`PA7` analog carbon-pill keys** (`OP1–GLO` `8` top + `Keys 1–12` `3 × 4` bottom) to continuous `0–100%` expressive pressure stepping in calibrated equal-ratio physical lumen groups (`65% → 40% → 15%` at rest, `100%` when squeezed or `HOLD`-latched). Standardize exact functional brightness levels (`0%` inactive/silent, `15%` assigned & ready, `50%` sub-audio `< 20 Hz` or `EDITED` scene, `80%` audible `MUTE`/`SOLO` voice ceiling, `100%` `SHIFT`/active beacon): `MONO = HOLD` (`15%` rest, `100%` latched), `POLY = TAP TEMPO` (`0%` dark and inert in `1/3` & `2/3`; quarter-note beat pulse & live tap only in `3/3 RHYTHM LAB`), `11th = Universal SHIFT` (`100%` permanently lit corner beacon), `HOME = 100%` on all Home screens (including Clean Scope, `15%` on other pages), and `Keys 13–16 = MUTE 1–4 / SHIFT + SOLO 1–4` showing **steady oscillator output status** (`80%` max audible `>= 20 Hz` so `SHIFT` stays brightest, `50%` sub-audio `< 20 Hz`, `0%` silent/muted — never dancing with LFOs or gates).
  2. **Single-State `MUTE` / `SHIFT + SOLO` (Pre-Solo Mix Memory & Release-Order Guard):** Remove the separate `SOLO MODE` row from `GLOBAL CONFIG` (`9` rows, renaming the credits row to **`ABOUT`** and adding `"Built with Google Antigravity"`, paired with a `2.00 s` cold-boot `RUIDO` CRT intro; 16-byte settings record preserved). `MUTE MODE` (`MOMENTARY` default / `LATCHING`) governs `Keys 13–16` as one unified voice-mute state: in `MOMENTARY`, `Key 13–16` mutes while held and `SHIFT + Key 13–16` isolates while held; in `LATCHING`, unshifted `Key 13–16` toggles voice mute (`1` tap, including direct unmuting after Solo), while `SHIFT + Key 13–16` performs **Exclusive Isolate** (saving the 4-bit pre-Solo mix on entry, walking cleanly across `13 -> 14 -> 15` or chords, and restoring the saved pre-Solo mix when tapping the already-isolated voice under `SHIFT`), with release-order protection if `SHIFT` lifts first.
  3. **Universal `SHIFT` Layer (`TEMPO · KEYBOARD · SCENE · SOLO` Across Encoders & Keys):** Holding `SHIFT` displays a hardware-accurate Full-Chassis `FM-1` silhouette (`2 × 2` left encoders + `[OCT- | OCT+]`, `1:1` square screen left of center, `4` top-aligned right knobs + `2 × 6` button bezel, and one continuous full-width 27-key stadium well) and illuminates the universal `SHIFT` controls across all 3 models: **`TEMPO`** on `ALGORITHM` (`20–3,000 BPM`, keeping unshifted `ALGORITHM` as **`RAIL COUPLING`** across all 3 models), **`KEYBOARD 1–3`** on **`SELECT` encoder** & **`OP1–OP3`** (direct model selection without opening `SEL`, `15%` steady on inactive models and a slow `0.40 Hz` `15% ↔ 100%` pulse on the active model snapping immediately to `100%` on selection), **`SCENE 1–4`** on **`PRESETS` encoder** & `Keys 9–12` (circular scene morphing, recall, `50%` Home Base Snap-Back, chord blend, and `PA7` pressure morph—mirroring live scene weights onto `Keys 9–12` LEDs and rendering the 4-Node Circular Scene Loop graphic cleanly on both `SEL` and `SHIFT`), and **`SOLO 1–4`** on `Keys 13–16`.
  4. **Screen `Line` vs. `Dot` Language, Clean Labels & Decoupled `SHUFFLE` / `PING-PONG`:** Keep crisp, uniform active capsule outlines across `SEL`, `SHIFT`, and `SAVE` (ghosting only genuinely inactive keys), rendering **vertical diffuser lines** (with upward squeeze fill) inside pressure pads and **centered optical dots** inside switches, removing the unnecessary horizontal LFO bracket line from `EDIT -> PAN`, and using concise zone legends (`DRIVE · FILTER · DETUNE`, `MOD · SYNC · BENDS`, `PUMP · GATE · GROOVE`) and parameter + value readouts free of prose sentences. Populate the 20 expressive pads with `OP5 HIGH PASS`, `PIT DETUNE`, and `Keys 9–12 DUTY 1–4` in `1/3`; `PIT SUB-XOR` and `GLO FEEDBACK` in `2/3`; and pure timing **`SHUFFLE`** (`OP6`), stereo **`PING-PONG`** (`PIT`), and `x2–x8` ratchet **`ROLL`** (`GLO`) in `3/3`.
- **Consequences:** Aligns software 1-to-1 with physical `PA8`/`PA7` vs. `PA6` sensing and hardware LED gamma response, eliminates phantom tap-tempo triggers outside `RHYTHM LAB`, unifies `MUTE` and `SOLO` without hidden dual-state conflicts, adds instant `SHIFT + OP1–OP3` keyboard switching, clearly differentiates pressure pads from switches on screen, and fits cleanly within Core 1 SRAM (`47.94 KiB < 48 KiB`) and Core 0 controller (`2,040 B < 2 KiB`) / XIP Flash (`< 600 KiB`) budgets.

## ADR-056: Strict 3-Zone Screen Contract, Graphical Scene UX (`15% / 40% / 80%` & Pure-Blinking `SAVE`/`DELETE`), Centralized `SHIFT` Layer, Shared Per-Oscillator `FX + LFO A/B` Modulator Dots, `LATCH` Nomenclature & Unified Unassigned Feedback
- **Date:** 2026-11-16 · **Status:** Implemented (refines screen zoning, Scene/SHIFT/LED UX, modulator indicators, and nomenclature in ADR-054/ADR-055)
- **Context:** Hands-on hardware evaluation on `FM-1_960` exposed seven UI/UX inconsistencies: (1) `SHIFT` and `SEL` duplicated active parameter readouts at both the top and bottom of the screen instead of honoring a single Top-Bar authority; (2) `SHIFT` placed `"KEYBOARD"` beneath bottom white keys while `OP1–OP3` sit on the top row, lit upper-deck encoders without legends, did not update keyboard legends when turning `SHIFT + SELECT`, and got kicked out of `SHIFT` when turning `SHIFT + PRESETS`; (3) `LATCH` (`MONO`) and `TAP` (`POLY` in `RHYTHM LAB`) lacked top-row legends on `SEL`, while `"KEY HOLD"` conflicted with `LATCH` terminology; (4) Scene feedback mixed ASCII `"1 -> 2"` / `"PRESSURE MIX"`, a floating 4-node loop track, and an ambiguous `"EDITED"` state; (5) `SAVE` / `DELETE` mixed resting keyboard brightnesses onto non-scene keys instead of focusing strictly on the blinking target keys; (6) the `HOME` per-oscillator `FX + LFO A/B` dots were cramped against the bottom bezel, drove `FX` with a synthetic sine wave, collapsed `LFO A/B` into one global sum, and were missing from `EDIT -> LEVEL` and `EDIT -> PAN`; and (7) unassigned controls under `SHIFT` showed `"UNASSIGNED"` text instead of the standard unassigned CRT ripple + LED glint, while `ABOUT` had wider left margin than `GLOBAL CONFIG` rows and carried an unnecessary `"Built with Google Antigravity"` line.
- **Decision:**
  1. **Strict 3-Zone Screen Contract, Top-Bar Held-Key Readouts & Full-Width Bottom Pressure Bar:** Establish three non-overlapping screen responsibilities across every view: **Top Bar** = sole authority for parameter titles, `OSC 1..4` qualifiers, live values, `FINE`/`COARSE` rate badges, and the horizontal spinning Scene number disk + 4-dot Scene strip (including `<NAME> OSC 1..4` + `<VALUE>` while holding any active key on `SEL`); **Center Stage** = live waveform, active parameter graph, menu, or spatial hardware diagram (`RAIL COUPLING` replaces all `HOME` screens `Clean Scope`, `FREQUENCY`, `DUTY`, and `DRIFT` for `2.2 s` while non-Home pages stay pinned); **Bottom Strip** = strictly `KNOB 1–4` optical gauges + modulator dots, or calm static hints on `SEL`, `SHIFT`, and `SAVE`, replaced dynamically when holding an active key by a **full-width (`216 px`) horizontal pressure bar** (for analog pressure pads) directly above a clean 1-line circuit description (free of `"THIS OSC"` phrasing and never duplicating Top-Bar readouts). Left-align `ABOUT` body lines to the exact `GLOBAL CONFIG` row margin and remove `"Built with Google Antigravity"`.
  2. **Top-Bar Spinning Scene Number Disk, Minimal 4-Key `PRESETS` Screen, `15% / 40% / 80%` Scene LED Ladder & Pure-Blinking `SAVE`/`DELETE`:** Retire `"EDITED"`, `"SCENE MORPH"`, ASCII `"1 -> 2"` / `"PRESSURE MIX"`, the floating 4-node loop track, and unshifted `Keys 9–12` LED overrides; Scene slots use strictly **`ACTIVE`**, **`STORED`**, and **`EMPTY`**. Reserve `0%` exclusively for unassigned controls and silent voices, assigning Scene keys (`Keys 9–12` under `SHIFT`) to **`EMPTY = 15%`**, **`STORED = 40%`**, and **`ACTIVE = 80%`** (morphing `40%..80%` by live weight so `SHIFT = 100%` stays brightest, while physical `Keys 9–12` LEDs stay on their active keyboard's rest LEDs when `SHIFT` is not held). In the **Top Bar**, pair a number-only **Horizontal Spinning Scene Disk** (`1, 2, 3, 4` rotating smoothly with cylindrical foreshortening directly in the `"X"` slot of **`SCENE X`**) with the right-side **4-Dot Graphical Scene Strip**. When turning `PRESETS` on **Clean Scope**, momentarily show **only the 4 Scene Keys (`1–4`)** with uniform full-height LED diffuser slits (zero rising pressure bars, zero other keyboard keys) above calm 2-line `SHIFT` location guidance (`HOLD SHIFT FOR SCENE KEYS` / `SHIFT = LIT TOP-RIGHT KEY`), while keeping open parameter graphs (`HOME`, `FX`, `LFO`, `LEVEL`, `PAN`) visible so curves physically morph live. When `SAVE` (`STORE` `1/2` or `DELETE` `2/2`) is open, hold all 23 non-target keys at **`0%` dark** so the keybed conveys strictly the blinking target function (`2.5 Hz` in Store, `5.0 Hz` on occupied slots in Delete).
  3. **Always-On Full-Chassis `SHIFT` Layer, Zero Label Backgrounds & Consistent `220 ms` Keyboard Wave:** Keep the unmodified `1.75:1` Full-Chassis `SHIFT` Map on screen **`100%` of the time while `SHIFT` is held** (never exiting `SHIFT` on `SHIFT + SELECT`, `SHIFT + PRESETS`, or `SHIFT + ALGORITHM`). Ghost non-`SHIFT` hardware (`MASTER`, `PRESETS`, `KNOB 1–4`, `2×6` buttons, unassigned keys) dark and light/label strictly controls with dedicated `SHIFT` functions directly over the dim ghosted chassis (zero pill/knockout backgrounds): **`SELECT` → `KEYBOARD`**, **`ALGORITHM` → `TEMPO`**, **`KEYBOARD`** centered directly above `OP1–OP3` with downward bracket (zero bottom legs), and **`SCENE`** / **`SOLO`** below `Keys 9–12` / `Keys 13–16`—firing the shared `220 ms` kinetic LED wave across physical and on-screen keys on every keyboard switch (`SEL`, `SELECT`, `SHIFT + SELECT`, and `SHIFT + OP1–OP3`).
  4. **Target-Matched Per-Oscillator `FX + LFO A/B` 3-Dot Modulator Component (`HOME`, `LEVEL`, `PAN`):** Render the identical 3-circle modulator trio (`FX`, `LFO A`, `LFO B`) with breathing room above the bottom bezel across `HOME` (`FREQUENCY`, `DUTY`, `DRIFT`), `EDIT -> LEVEL`, and stereo `EDIT -> PAN`—keeping inactive dots clearly visible (`~14%`), driving `FX` steadily from that oscillator's live filter engagement across the voice strip, and animating `LFO A` / `LFO B` **strictly on the parameter screen that LFO directly targets** (`FREQ` on `FREQUENCY`, `DUTY` on `DUTY`, none on `DRIFT`, `LEVEL` on `LEVEL`, `PAN` on `PAN`).
  5. **`LATCH` & `TAP` Legends, Tightened Top-Row Group Headers & Unified Unassigned Animation:** Standardize on **`LATCH`** (replacing `"KEY HOLD"` everywhere), render top-row **`LATCH`** (all 3 keyboards) and **`TAP`** (`RHYTHM LAB`) callouts above the Right 7-Switch Bay on `SEL`, and tighten all 3 keyboards' top-row group headers so every header describes all keys in its cluster (**`TONE · FILTER · WIDTH`**, **`MOD · LOCK · BENDS`**, **`PULSE · GATE · FX`**). Retire `"UNASSIGNED"` header text so every `0%` unassigned control across the instrument (`ENV/ARP/SEQ/REC`, unassigned keys/switches under `SHIFT`, `TAP` outside `RHYTHM LAB`, and dark keys during `SAVE`/`DELETE`) triggers the identical non-disruptive CRT scanline ripple + `120 ms` `0% -> 15% -> 0%` local LED glint.
- **Consequences:** Eliminates duplicate top/bottom readouts, `SHIFT` mode exits, 1-detent Scene number flashes, misleading off-target LFO dots, and single-key group names while preserving 1-line key effect descriptions and keeping static RAM compact (`sizeof(RuidoController) = 2,024 B`, `sizeof(ScopeHudRenderer) = 124,752 B`).

## ADR-057: `v0.7.0` Milestone — Kinetic 4-Scene Factory Voicing (`Boot + 3 Keyboard Showcases`) & Playability-First `GLOBAL CONFIG` Ordering
- **Date:** 2026-11-16 · **Status:** Implemented (`v0.7.0`)
- **Context:** Hardware playtesting surfaced two polish opportunities ahead of tagging the `v0.7.0` milestone: (1) the initial `Scene 1` boot patch used glacial `0.04–0.11 Hz` LFOs and a sub-`78 Hz` anchor that looked slow on the `240x240` oscilloscope, whereas `Scenes 2–4` could serve as purpose-voiced companions for each of the 3 performance keyboards in exact `1/3 -> 2/3 -> 3/3` order; and (2) in `GLOBAL CONFIG`, `Key Sensitivity` sat down at Row 7 below the 6-row initial viewport fold, splitting tactile keybed settings across opposite ends of the menu.
- **Decision:**
  1. **`v0.7.0` 4-Scene Factory Bank (`Scene 1` Boot Cinema + `Scenes 2–4` Mapped `1-to-1` to Keyboards `1/3..3/3`):**
