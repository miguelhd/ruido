Two-Hand Control Surface & Hardware Auditioning

  • Left-Hand Momentary Holds: Hold 1–4 (OP1–4 Osc 1–4 Filter), 5 (OP5 Global Filter), 6 (OP6 Panning), G (GLO Amplitude), or 0 (Scope Freeze) while turning KNOB1–4 — all modifier black keys are strictly momentary and return immediately on release.
  • Per-Voice Analog LFOs (LFO / SELECT): Tap LFO (L) to toggle the per-voice LFO page, step SELECT (, / .) across OSC 1–4 and ALL 4, and shape TARGET | SHAPE | RATE | DEPTH on KNOB1–4 (H or Esc exits back to the scope while LFOs keep modulating).
  • 3-Speed Encoders (OCT- / OCT+): Hold OCT- ([ or -) for Fine sub-cent tuning, or hold OCT+ (] or =) for Fast full-range sweeps in ~1–1.5 turns; release returns immediately to Standard rate.
  • Carbon-Pill Expression (SEL Linked Brightness) & USB-MIDI: Drag upward on White Keys 5–8 (or scroll wheel / ↑↓ while holding A S D F) to sweep MS-20 filter formant & dual resonance (or Vactrol LPG when muted). Tap SEL (Q) to toggle Keys 5–8 into Pitch Bend — SEL and Keys 5–8 step their LED brightness in unison so their link is always visible.
