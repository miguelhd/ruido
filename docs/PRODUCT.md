# Ruido — Product & UX Specification

## 1. Vision & Musical Identity

**Ruido** transforms the pocket **M-VAVE FM-1** hardware into a dedicated, boutique **4-oscillator CMOS Schmitt-trigger stereo drone instrument**.

- **Primary Acoustic Reference:** Classic DIY analog Schmitt-trigger relaxation oscillator synths (`CD40106` / `74HC14` style)—organic capacitor tilt, micro-thermal drift, shared power-rail phase pulling, and starved-rail hard-sync tearing—paired with feral **Korg MS-20 `Rev 2 -> Korg35` dual `HPF -> LPF` silicon-limited resonant filters** and **ESP-style asymmetric pre-filter transistor overdrive**.
- **Primary Studio Role:** A pristine-to-feral, harmonically rich, zero-aliasing stereo sound source with **zero artificial musical clamps**, designed specifically to feed **external guitar/synth effects pedals** (fuzz, wavefolders, BBD delays, reverbs, granular) and **Eurorack modular rigs**.

### Core Design Values (In Priority Order)
1. **Great Sound & Unclamped Extremes:** Spend every spare CPU cycle on analog-faithful oscillator physics, `4x` oversampling, nonlinear Zero-Delay Feedback filtering, and clickless slewing—imposing safety limits strictly for numerical/hardware protection (anti-aliasing, zero `NaN`/DC runaway, DAC rail safety), never to domesticate musical range.
2. **Immediate, Ergonomic UX with Judicious Pages:** Default idle state is always live waveform + `KNOB1-4 = Osc 1-4 Frequency`, with momentary encoder rate holds on the left (`OCT-/OCT+`), all 27 silicone keys acting as non-destructive momentary performance punch-ins so forearms never cross over the display, a stateless non-modal keyboard model selector on `SEL` + `SELECT`, and judicious single-level pages on the right-side `2x6` button grid (`FX` MS-20 Filters, 2-page `EDIT` `DUTY · DRIFT · LEVEL` -> `PAN`, `LFO` modulation, and `GLO` `GLOBAL CONFIG`, always one tap from `HOME`).
3. **Useful Waveform Visuals:** An always-on oscilloscope that serves as an actual acoustic tuning and gain-staging instrument, with minimal controls gently fading in over the wave only while touched or viewing a focused page.

## 2. Voice & Signal Architecture (User-Facing)

Ruido consists of **4 identical mono voices** (`Osc 1..4`) mixed in stereo through a subtle nonlinear master bus:

```text
[Schmitt Pulse/Square Osc 1..4 (DUTY + 1/f DRIFT)] ──> [Pre-Filter Drive 1.0x..4.5x] ──> [MS-20 Dual HPF -> LPF 1..4] ──> (VCA + Gate + Pan) ──> [Master Sag] ──> L/R Out
                       ▲                                                                                                                             │
                       └────────────────────────── Shared Power-Rail Coupling & Starved-Rail Sync (`ALGORITHM`: 0-100%) ─────────────────────────────┘
```

### Per-Oscillator Controls (`Osc 1..4`)
- **Frequency:** Unbroken continuous stepless sweep from **Sub-Cycle Pulses to Ultrasonic Edge (`0.05 Hz - 20,000 Hz`)** (`1.5 Hz` RC coupling corner preserving `-0.04 dB` seismic `10–30 Hz` sub-bass weight), using **strictly linear 1:1 X4 quadrature scaling (`96 steps/turn`, zero velocity acceleration)** + **`C^1` critically-damped log-octave smoothing**:
  - *`FINE` (`Hold OCT-`):* Surgical `~0.66 cents/step` (`~63 cents/turn`) precision for locking slow phase beating and harmonic intervals.
  - *`STANDARD` (Released Default):* Predictable linear `0.0387 oct/step` (`~3.7 oct/turn`) sweep regardless of hand speed.
  - *`COARSE` (`Hold OCT+`):* Rapid end-to-end `0.05 Hz - 20,000 Hz` (`18.6`-octave) sweep in **`~2.0` turns** (`0.096 oct/step`).
- **Pulse Duty (`EDIT -> DUTY`, `5-95%`):** Continuous piecewise-slope PolyBLEP CMOS Schmitt comparator duty-cycle control (`50%` square default, sweeping from `5%` razor-thin nasal pulse through `50%` hollow square to `95%` wide pulse with exact fundamental pitch invariance and `< -109 dBc` alias suppression).
- **Thermal Pitch Drift (`EDIT -> DRIFT`, `0-100%`):** Per-oscillator `1/f` analog RC thermal pitch instability (`10%` / `~±2.2 cents` organic default; `0%` = **`LOCKED`** `0.00 cents` crystal phase-lock up to `100%` = `~±120 cents` tape-warble / unstable transistor swarm).
- **Dual MS-20 `Rev 2 -> Korg35` Filter (`HPF -> LPF` in series across `2.0 Hz - 28,000 Hz`, Zero Smart-Park Muting):**
  - **HP Cutoff (`KNOB1`):** `2.0 Hz` (`0%` RES parked flat/transparent) up to `28,000 Hz`, engaging warm `LM13600` OTA saturation across all 4 filter poles even at `0%` resonance.
  - **HP Resonance (`KNOB2`):** Unmuted full-range High-Pass Peak (`0-100%`)—`0-60%` models creamy **`Rev 2`** (`LM13600` OTA + `1S1588`, `k <= 3.60`), while `60-100%` morphs continuously into feral early **`Korg35`** (`k` up to `6.80`, tighter diode knees, asymmetric Stage-2 BJT/OTA rails), self-oscillating all the way down to `2 Hz` infrasonic throb with exact `0 Hz` capacitive DC rejection.
  - **LP Cutoff (`KNOB3`):** `2.0 Hz` up to `28,000 Hz` (`0%` RES parked flat/wide-open), rounding square edges with creamy `LM13600` OTA 2nd/3rd harmonic warmth and extending self-oscillation past `20 kHz`.
  - **LP Resonance (`KNOB4`):** Unmuted full-range Low-Pass Peak (`0-100%`, `Rev 2` `0-60%` -> `Korg35` `60-100%` up to `k = 6.80`), unpadding `HPF -> LPF` inter-stage gain above `60%` so dual-resonant formants slam `IC6` into raw intermodulation screaming without bass-quenching.
- **Channel Level & MS-20 Pre-Filter Overdrive (`EDIT` Page 1 -> `LEVEL`, `0-100%`, Boot Default on `EDIT`):** Two-zone analog console/ESP gain law: `0-70%` = clean post-filter VCA attenuation (`1.0x` pre-drive; `65%` default); **`70-100%` = `1.0x -> 4.5x` asymmetric pre-filter MS-20 transistor overdrive** slamming `HPF -> LPF` into heavy 2nd/3rd harmonic saturation + `+4.1 dB` post-filter trim.
- **Stereo Pan (`EDIT` Page 2 -> `PAN`):** Equal-power (`sin`/`cos`) stereo placement from hard Left (`L100`) through Center (`C`) to hard Right (`R100`), or collapsed to Mono (`L == R`) when `MONO` output routing is active.

### Ergonomic Rules
1. **Unobstructed Screen & Two-Hand Split:** Left-hand encoder rate modifiers (`OCT-` / `OCT+`) live top-left, the entire 27-key silicone bed below operates as non-destructive momentary performance punch-ins, the right hand stays top-right on `KNOB1-4` and the `2x6` button grid, and the center `240x240` screen is never occluded by a crossed forearm.
2. **Right-Side `2x6` Button Grid = Non-Momentary Toggles, Stateless `SEL` Toast & Judicious Pages:** None of the `2x6` buttons directly below `KNOB1-4` are momentary holds; they act as latching toggles (`PLAY/STOP`, `HOME`, `SAVE`), a stateless non-modal keyboard model toast (`SEL`), or standardized pages (`FX` MS-20 Filters, 2-page `EDIT` `DUTY · DRIFT · LEVEL` -> `PAN`, `LFO` modulation, and `GLO` `GLOBAL CONFIG`).

### 3.2 Top-Left Knobs & `OCT-` / `OCT+` (Zero-Modifier Global Controls)
- **`MASTER` (Analog Pot):** Hardware master output level (`MASTER LEVEL` + `%` bar on HUD, anchored with `> 0.2%` cumulative deadband so slow analog sweeps wake the HUD reliably without ADC chatter).
- **`ALGORITHM` (Encoder):** Context-sensitive left-hand performance encoder:
  - **In `1/3 LIVE MIXER` & `2/3 CIRCUIT LAB`:** **Rail Coupling & Starved-Rail Chaos** (`0-45%` smooth `CD40106` shared-rail threshold pulling around the `25%` default <-> `45-100%` starved-CMOS voltage collapse: `[0.12x..4.5x]` phase pull, neighbor gate-load PWM duty warp, and band-limited PolyBLEP subharmonic hard-sync locking).
  - **In `3/3 RHYTHM LAB`:** **Logarithmic Master Tempo (`20–3,000 BPM`, `120 BPM` default)** — sweeps the internal master bar clock along a musical 3-gear logarithmic curve from slow `20 BPM` hypnotic pulse through audio-rate formant gating (`2.0` octaves/turn = `1` semitone/detent in `STANDARD` across `~3.6` one-handed turns, surgical `0.1 BPM` beat-matching while holding `OCT-` `FINE` directly below `ALGORITHM`, and `6.0` octaves/turn = `~1.2` turns full sweep while holding `OCT+` `COARSE`), displaying `TEMPO` with a log-scaled bar gauge and `BPM` readout in the top HUD header (`BPM EXT` when locked to incoming external `24 PPQN` MIDI Clock) and persisting your internal tempo in non-volatile device settings.
  - **When `GLOBAL CONFIG` (right-grid `GLO`) is open:** Steps (`1` option/detent) the highlighted menu row's option value so both menu navigation (`SELECT`) and option editing (`ALGORITHM`) stay paired on the left-hand encoder column while `KNOB 1–4` remain `Osc 1–4 Frequency`.
- **`SELECT` (Encoder):** Context-sensitive discrete left-hand mode/voice/row selector (`1` item per `4`-tick detent across all 3 gears):
  - While the **`SEL` Keyboard Model Guidance Screen** is open: steps across **`1/3 LIVE MIXER` · `2/3 CIRCUIT LAB` · `3/3 RHYTHM LAB`**, updating the on-screen rounded-capsule FM-1 keybed map and sweeping a kinetic varibright LED wave across all 27 keys.
  - When **`EDIT` Page 1 (3-Mode Oscillator Channel Strip)** is open: steps strictly in signal-flow order across **`DUTY` · `DRIFT` · `LEVEL` *(boot default)***, remembering your last selected mode across page re-opens.
  - When **`EDIT` Page 2 (Stereo Pan)** is open: switches **`OUTPUT ROUTING`** between **`STEREO`** and **`MONO`** right on the `PAN` page while keeping the live soundstage curve on screen.
  - When the **Per-Voice MS-20 Dual Filter Page** (`FX`) is open: steps strictly across **`OSC 1` · `OSC 2` · `OSC 3` · `OSC 4`**.
  - When the **Dual Per-Oscillator LFO Page** (`LFO`) is open: steps strictly across all 8 modulation slots **`LFO A OSC 1` · `LFO B OSC 1` · `LFO A OSC 2` · `LFO B OSC 2` · `LFO A OSC 3` · `LFO B OSC 3` · `LFO A OSC 4` · `LFO B OSC 4`**.
  - When **`GLOBAL CONFIG`** (right-grid `GLO`) is open: moves (`1` row/detent) the row cursor up/down the configuration list.
  - When **`SAVE`** is armed: toggles between **`STORE SCENE`** *(default on arm)* and **`DELETE SCENE`** before tapping `Scene Key 13–16`.
  - **At Idle / Live Scope (`OSCILLOSCOPE`):** Steps the oscilloscope horizontal scale across **6 notched stops (`4X` · `2X` · `AUTO` *(boot default)* · `1/2X` · `1/4X` · `1/8X`)**, zooming smoothly in on narrow `5%` PWM pulses, Serge wavefolder creases, and `CD4013` S&H steps (`2X / 4X`) or pulling out (`1/4X / 1/8X`) to inspect sub-bass cycles, multi-voice beating envelopes, and `RHYTHM LAB` chops while keeping the bottom of the screen `100%` unobstructed.
- **`PRESETS` (Encoder):** **Stepped 4-Slot Scene Selector (`SCENE 1–4`)** accessible across **all 3** keyboard models (`LIVE MIXER`, `CIRCUIT LAB`, and `RHYTHM LAB`, `1` occupied slot per `4`-tick detent, automatically skipping deleted `EMPTY` slots). Smoothly glides all 4 oscillators, filters, dual LFOs, and rail coupling in log-octave space into the chosen scene (`0.50 s` `STANDARD`, `1.80 s` `Hold OCT-` `FINE`, `0.10 s` `Hold OCT+` `COARSE`; stepping into the rail when `EDITED` glides cleanly back to the unedited scene).
- **`OCT-` / `OCT+` (Momentary Rate Modifiers — Top-Right Status Badge & `150 ms` Transition):** **Strictly momentary left-hand encoder rate modifiers** governing continuous endless rotary sweeps with **zero velocity acceleration** (100% deterministic 1:1 angle-to-parameter response at `96` X4 quadrature transitions per `360°` turn), freely chordable alongside any open right-grid page (discrete notched selectors like `SELECT` on `SEL`/`FX`/`EDIT`/`LFO`/`GLO`, `LFO TARGET`, and `GLOBAL CONFIG` rows remain `1` item per detent across all 3 gears):
  - *Released (Idle Default = `STANDARD`):* Both `OCT-` and `OCT+` rest at `15%` dim glow; full continuous parameter sweeps take an exact linear **`2.5-2.8` turns**.
  - *Hold `OCT-` -> **`FINE`** (`OCT-` LED `100%`):* Surgical fine-tuning while held (`0.66 cents/step` audio frequency, `3.0 cents/step` filter cutoff, `0.09%/step` linear controls).
  - *Hold `OCT+` -> **`COARSE`** (`OCT+` LED `100%`):* Rapid performance sweep while held—covering the **entire `0-100%` / `0.05 Hz-20 kHz` / `2 Hz-28 kHz` sweep in `~1.3-2.0` turns**.
  - *Anchored Left Title + Top-Right `FINE` / `COARSE` Transition:* Holding `OCT-` or `OCT+` **never moves or replaces the active page/tool title on the top-left** (`FREQUENCY` when held from idle). Instead, the **top-right status slot** smoothly crossfades over **`150 ms`** into **`FINE`** or **`COARSE`** (with zero underline or divider rules)—preserving live precision values alongside the badge (`FINE · 110.04 Hz` on `FREQUENCY`, `FINE · 25%` on `RAIL COUPLING`)—and smoothly dissolves back on release.
  - *Release:* Immediately returns to `STANDARD` rate (`15%` rest glow); if a knob was turned during the hold, its remaining `1.5 s` readout toast completes before dissolving in `150 ms` (or fades out immediately in `150 ms` if `OCT-/+` was tapped alone).

### 3.3 Right-Panel `2x6` Function Buttons (Latching Toggles & Focused Pages)
- **`PLAY/STOP`:** **Audio Output** toggle (`ON` / `OFF`, clickless fade to silence and back for safe cable patching). Drives the hardware's dual-channel `PLAY/STOP` LEDs by action affordance: steady **Green** (`PLAY` LED) when quiet (`OFF`), steady **Red** (`STOP` LED) when sound is `ON`, crossfading smoothly between them.
- **`HOME`:** **Toggle Active HUD Off <-> Latch `FREQUENCY` HUD** — when any page, modifier, or transient toast is active, pressing `HOME` hides the active HUD and dissolves smoothly over **`150 ms`** to the pure edge-to-edge waveform (`12%` rest LED, `12%–35%` ambient fade while other HUDs are visible); when no HUD is active, pressing `HOME` latches the **`FREQUENCY`** HUD open (`FREQUENCY` / `FREQUENCY OSC 1..4`, bottom `OSC 1..4` `Hz` gauges, and per-oscillator `FX` + `LFO A/B` status indicators over the `100%` undimmed live scope, `100%` `HOME` LED) until tapped again or switched to another page.
- **`SAVE`:** Arms/cancels **Scene Store or Delete** (`100%` `SAVE` LED across any keyboard model). Arming starts on **`STORE SCENE · TAP SCENE 1-4`** (pulsing all 4 `Keys 13–16` so tapping `Key 13–16` stores the current patch into `SCENE 1–4` with a `SAVED` toast); turning **`SELECT`** while armed toggles to **`DELETE SCENE · TAP SCENE 1-4`** (pulsing only occupied scene keys so tapping `Key 13–16` clears that slot into a persistent `EMPTY` state without interrupting live audio). Tapping `SAVE` a second time or pressing `HOME` cancels.
- **`FX`:** Opens/closes the **Per-Voice MS-20 Dual Filter Page** (see §3.4 below); LED glows at **`15%` rest** when all active voices' filters are parked flat (`HPF = 2.0 Hz`, `LPF = 28 kHz`, `0%` resonance), breathes subtly in the tertiary range (**`21%–37%`**, matching the `HOME` filter status dots) whenever any unmuted or active voice has filter cutoff or resonance engaged while closed, and lights to **`100%`** while the page is open.
- **`SEL`:** Opens/closes the **Keyboard Model Status & Guidance Screen** (`1/3..3/3`; see §3.4 below) while keeping `KNOB 1–4` directly bound to `OSC 1–4` Frequency; LED glows steadily at **`15%` rest** and lights to **`100%`** while the `SEL` screen is open.
- **`EDIT`:** Cycles through the **2-Page Oscillator Channel & Spatial Strip** (`1st tap` = Page 1 `DUTY · DRIFT · LEVEL`, `2nd tap` = Page 2 `PAN`, `3rd tap` or `HOME` = Close; see §3.4 below); LED glows at **`15%` rest** (scaling dynamically with overdrive/stereo spread when closed) and lights to **`100%`** across both `EDIT` Page 1 and Page 2.
- **`LFO`:** Opens/closes the **Dual Per-Oscillator LFO Page** (see §3.4 below); LED glows at **`15%` rest** when all 8 LFOs (`LFO A/B` across `OSC 1..4`) are at `0%` depth, breathes subtly in the tertiary range (**`21%–37%`**, matching the `HOME` LFO status dots) with active LFO modulation when closed, and lights to **`100%`** while the page is open.
- **`GLO` (Right Grid under `KNOB 1–4`):** Opens/closes the scalable **`GLOBAL CONFIG`** menu (`15%` steady rest LED, `100%` while open; see §3.4 below).
- **Unconnected Tactile Switches (`ENV`, `ARP`, `SEQ`, `REC`):** LEDs stay **`0%` completely unlit** at rest (`REC` is physically a single-color **Red** LED on the FM-1 PCB). Tapping any of the 4 switches **never** closes open pages, disarms `SAVE`, alters audio, or puts placeholder text on screen—instead kicking the `240x240` CRT scanline raster sideways (`±10–13 px` damped horizontal sync recoil + scanline shear over `~180 ms` with black-blanked edges, like tapping the side of a small CRT monitor) while bringing that switch's LED up to the `15%` steady glow of assigned buttons before fading back to `0%` dark in `120 ms`.

### 3.4 Right-Grid Pages & Keyboard Model Guidance (`FX`, `SEL`, `EDIT`, `LFO`, `GLOBAL CONFIG`)

#### Per-Voice MS-20 Dual Filter Page (`FX` — `ADR-029`)
- **Enter / Exit:** Tap **`FX`** (top-left switch of the `2x6` grid below `KNOB 1`) to open/close the latching MS-20 Dual Filter page (`FILTER OSC 1..4` with `12 dB HP -> LP` right header); mutually exclusive with `SEL`, `EDIT`, `LFO`, `GLOBAL CONFIG`, and `SAVE`, and dismissed in `150 ms` by a second `FX` tap or **`HOME`** (momentary 27-key punch-ins temporarily override and return seamlessly on release).
- **`SELECT` Encoder (Left Hand):** Steps (`1` item/detent, clamped `0..3` with zero windup) strictly across **`OSC 1` · `OSC 2` · `OSC 3` · `OSC 4`**, remembering the last focused voice across page re-opens.
- **`KNOB 1–4` (`HPF | HP RES | LPF | LP RES`):** Adjust High-Pass Cutoff (`2 Hz–28 kHz`), High-Pass Resonance (`0–100%`), Low-Pass Cutoff (`2 Hz–28 kHz`), and Low-Pass Resonance (`0–100%`) for the selected voice.
- **Live Full-Screen MS-20 Passband Visualization:** Spans edge-to-edge across the display (`2 Hz–28 kHz`, anchored to the shared unipolar graph baseline) over a dimmed live waveform backdrop, rendering the `0 dB` passband reference, dual `Korg35` resonant peaks, and real-time Filter LFO + Keyboard Formant modulation for the focused voice.

#### Keyboard Model Status & Guidance Screen (`SEL` + `SELECT` — `ADR-036`, `ADR-037`, `ADR-038`, `ADR-039`)
- **Inspect / Dismiss (`SEL` Button):** Tapping **`SEL`** closes any other page and opens a persistent **Keyboard Model Status & Guidance** screen (`KEYBOARD 1/3..3/3`, `SEL` LED `100%`) so you can inspect the swappable interaction model carousel (`< LIVE MIXER >`, `< CIRCUIT LAB >`, `< RHYTHM LAB >`), its anatomically accurate rounded-capsule FM-1 keybed map (short upper slits on `OP1–POLY`, full-height slit on the `11th` key, and `4·3·4·3·2` registration dots), its live 7-tier varibright LED zones, and pressure-sensitivity guidance (`TURN SELECT TO SWAP MODEL` + `KEYS ARE PRESSURE SENSITIVE`) at your own pace. After `5 s` idle, the entire illustration and guidance slowly dim down to `50%` brightness so the live waveform shines through clearly, and smoothly return to `100%` regular brightness whenever you turn `SELECT` (or `ALGORITHM`), while **`KNOB 1–4` remain directly mapped to `OSC 1–4` Frequency** and pressing any key jumps live into its native performance view before returning cleanly on release. Tapping `SEL` a second time or pressing `HOME` dissolves the screen in `150 ms`; tapping `SEL` never cycles models on its own.
- **Swap Model (`SELECT` Encoder):** Turning the left-hand **`SELECT`** encoder while the `SEL` screen is open steps between the 3 keyboard models, updates the on-screen zone legends and slit brightness 1-to-1 with the physical hardware LEDs, and sweeps a signature varibright LED wave across all 27 silicone keys.

#### 2-Page Oscillator Channel & Spatial Strip (`EDIT` — `ADR-028`, `ADR-030`, `ADR-032`, `ADR-033`, `ADR-036`)
- **3-Step Cycle (`Page 1 -> Page 2 -> Close`):**
  1. **`1st Tap` (`EDIT` Page 1 — Channel Strip `DUTY · DRIFT · LEVEL`):** Opens at your remembered channel mode (**`LEVEL`** on boot). Left-hand **`SELECT`** steps (`1` mode/detent in signal-flow order) across **`DUTY`** (`5–95%` piecewise-slope PolyBLEP CMOS Schmitt pulse duty, `50%` `SQUARE` default), **`DRIFT`** (`0–100%` `1/f` analog thermal pitch wander, `10%` `~±2.2c` default, `0%` = `LOCKED` `0.00c` -> `100%` = `~±120c`), and **`LEVEL`** (`0–70%` clean post-filter VCA -> **`70–100%` `1.0x..4.5x` MS-20 pre-filter input overdrive**; turning CCW to the `0%` rail without active `LEVEL` LFO engages `MUTED`, while turning CW fades in smoothly from `0%` and clears mute).
  2. **`2nd Tap` (`EDIT` Page 2 — Stereo Pan `PAN`):** Advances to per-oscillator Stereo Panning (`PAN`). Left-hand **`SELECT`** switches **`OUTPUT ROUTING`** between **`STEREO`** (`CCW`) and **`MONO`** (`CW`) right on the `PAN` page while keeping the live soundstage curve on screen. In `STEREO`, **`KNOB 1–4`** adjust equal-power placement (`L100..C..R100`) per oscillator and spotlight that voice's curve; in **`MONO`**, `KNOB 1–4` lock cleanly and the bottom bay displays **`OUTPUT ROUTING SET TO MONO`**.
  3. **`3rd Tap` (or `HOME` Anytime):** Closes `EDIT` and dissolves in `150 ms` back to the pure live waveform (`EDIT` LED stays `100%` across both Page 1 and Page 2).
- **Live Full-Screen Visualizations:** Page 1 aligns 4 vertical optical columns directly above `KNOB 1–4` (`DUTY` single-cycle CMOS pulse cells, `DRIFT` thermal excursion brackets + wander beacons, and `LEVEL` clean VCA phosphor stems topped above `70%` by live-audio triangular overdrive flare); Page 2 renders the edge-to-edge Stereo Panorama Curve (`4` Cauchy-Lorentzian acoustic localization bells with psychoacoustic bass-to-treble width, `LFO -> PAN` excursion brackets, and automatic centered convergence in `MONO`).

#### Dual Per-Oscillator LFO Page (`LFO A/B OSC 1..4` — `ADR-022`, `ADR-027`, `ADR-028`, `ADR-031`, `ADR-032`)
Each of the 4 voices (`Osc 1..4`) owns **2 dedicated LFOs (`LFO A` + `LFO B` = `8` total)** matching its dedicated MS-20 filter, VCA/overdrive, and panner:
- **Enter / Exit:** Tap **`LFO`** (right-grid switch) to open/close the LFO Page (`LFO A OSC 1` .. `LFO B OSC 4` showing `ACTIVE` / `BYPASSED`); tap **`LFO`** again or **`HOME`** to return to the pure scope in `150 ms` while active LFOs continue running.
- **`SELECT` Encoder (Left Hand):** Steps (`1` slot/detent, clamped `0..7` with zero windup) across all 8 modulation slots **`LFO A OSC 1` · `LFO B OSC 1` · `LFO A OSC 2` · `LFO B OSC 2` · `LFO A OSC 3` · `LFO B OSC 3` · `LFO A OSC 4` · `LFO B OSC 4`**, remembering the last focused slot across page re-opens.
- **`KNOB 1–4` (`TARGET | SHAPE | RATE | DEPTH` — Setup Left, Perform Right):**

## 5. Explicit Non-Goals (v1 Scope Guardrails)

To keep Ruido focused, reliable, and sonically uncompromising, **v1 explicitly excludes**:
- Multi-track step sequencers, piano rolls, or arpeggiators.
- Chromatic MIDI polyphony / voice-stealing envelopes.
- Onboard time-based FX (reverb, delay, chorus)—Ruido is the **dry analog-style source** feeding your external pedals and Eurorack modules.
- Deep nested sub-menus or file browsers (focused single-level pages on right-grid buttons—`FX`, `SEL`, `EDIT`, `LFO`, and `GLO` Config—are used judiciously, always instant-exit via `HOME`).
